## Procopio and Rubino are not the same kind of evidence

Both are cited at the two indefinite-causal-order sites, but the citation
currently reads as two demonstrations of one thing. They are not.

Procopio et al. (Nat. Commun. 6:7913, 2015) realise the quantum switch: a
superposition of gate orders. Rubino et al. (Sci. Adv. 3:e1602589, 2017)
measure a *causal witness*, certifying causal non-separability.

That difference is worth a clause, because Rubino's is a record certifying
indefiniteness -- the nearest thing in the literature to a counterexample to
"no experiment will ever hold a record of indefinite causal order"
(`predictions_section`, line 227). The claim survives it: what the witness
records is that the process was non-separable, not an indefinite order
persisting through amplification. Citing the hardest available case against
one's own prediction is stronger than citing two easy ones, but only if the
prose says so.
