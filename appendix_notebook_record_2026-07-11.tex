notebook, each rebuilt during the July 2026 audit from a fresh locked re-run combined with its
original write-up. The epistemic rule of the earlier appendices is inherited unchanged and stated
once: execution validates that the framework's structural claims have been \emph{faithfully
encoded} and that the encoded structures behave as asserted; whether the structures are the right

characterised noise --- and every hardware number below is quoted with its job identifier and its
raw partner.

\emph{Provenance.} \texttt{qiskit\_A\_ckm\_charged\_current.py} and

\emph{Claim.} The $W^{-}$ vertex extended by a generation qubit makes the first-rung CKM factors

\emph{Design.} Six wires, little-endian $(B_{\nu}, B_{e}, g, C_{q}, B_{q}, A_{q})$, depth four:
$R_{y}(2\cdot\tfrac29)$ on $g$; $X$ on $B_{q}$ (the $T_{3}$ raise); $H$ and CNOT forming the
lepton Bell pair. The factors are read from the generation-qubit marginal,
$\lvert V_{us}\rvert = \sqrt{P(g{=}1)}$, and the Bell witness $P(q_{1}{=}q_{0})$ from the same

\emph{Verified outputs.} Exact statevector: four basis states with amplitudes $\pm 0.689719$ and

seventeen strings; job identifier likewise uncaptured, recoverable as the account's most recent

\emph{Status.} The notebook's own epistemic header is the appendix-wide rule and predates it:

\emph{Claim.} The mass-relevant invariant --- the Bloch radius $r$ of one cut, the base
coordinate of the geodesic mass proxy --- responds at \emph{first order} in the admixture
amplitude, $r^{2} = \tfrac23\alpha^{2}\beta^{2} + \beta^{4}/9$, a $17.2\times$ enhancement over
incoherent mixing at $\beta^{2} = 0.02$, and is \emph{rigid} in the relative phase $\varphi$.
Feeds Open Problem~\ref{op:up-quark}'s enabling properties, constrained by Open
Problem~\ref{op:associator-geodesic}. Reframed 2026-07-11 after the class-discreteness
retirement: the prepared family is, generically, a probe path \emph{within} the GHZ class

the sedenion cross-algebra sector (\S\ref{sec:sedenion-announcement}).

\emph{Design.} \texttt{prepare\_state} of
$\alpha\lvert\mathrm{GHZ}\rangle + \beta e^{\mathrm{i}\varphi}\lvert\mathrm{W}\rangle$ on three
qubits; single-qubit tomography of the cut ($\langle X\rangle, \langle Y\rangle,
\langle Z\rangle$, $r$ the norm); sweep $\beta^{2} \in \{0.02, 0.05, 0.10, 0.50, 1.00\}$ at
$\varphi = 0$, with the rigidity check a twenty-five-point $\varphi$ sweep at

\emph{Verified outputs.} Exact table $r = 0.114504$, $0.178730$, $0.247207$, $0.440959$,

\emph{Status.} The notebook's prose predates the 2026-07-06 retirement and still motivates the
