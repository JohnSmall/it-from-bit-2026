\subsection{The rough edge, and where it is resolved}
\label{sec:sedenion-announcement}

The frustrated-pair count above is this programme's first account of the generation number, and it
carries a rough edge worth naming here even though its resolution belongs elsewhere. The reading
requires knowing \emph{which} qubit is the odd one out and what Cayley--Dickson rung it occupies,
while the canonical orbits of \S\ref{sec:canonical-orbits} are permutation-symmetric --- the state
treats the three qubits as interchangeable, and the label's rung-identity is imported from the tower
architecture rather than derived. Nothing is inconsistent; something is not quite located. The
diagnosis is that the generation index does not live on the three-qubit register at all but on the
next Cayley--Dickson rung, the sedenions $\mathbb{C}^{16} = \mathbb{C}\otimes\mathbb{S}$, whose
automorphism group gains exactly the finite factor $S_3$ that the octonions lack. That is the subject
of \S\ref{sec:sedenions-generations}, where the count becomes the order of an $S_3$-orbit, the
register account is recovered as its shadow, and the reason the fourth rung supplies a family label
and never a fourth force is given. The convergence of this route with the independent
Clifford-algebraic constructions of Gresnigt and collaborators, and with Szangolies's own closing
development, is set out there.
