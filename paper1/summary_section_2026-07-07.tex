\section{Summary of Results}
\label{sec:summary}

The contribution of this paper is not, for the most part, new theorems. It is a chain of new
identifications between existing ones: Lawvere's fixed-point theorem identified with the
situation of a system describing itself; the analytic continuation of Shannon's information
measure identified with the origin of complex amplitudes; Lamperti's isometry classification and
Hardy's axiom identified as two roads to one metric; the Frobenius classification of copying
structures identified with the boundary of the classical; Hurwitz's and Adams' termination
theorems identified with the end of the ladder of forces; the Hopf fibrations identified with
the state spaces the ladder equips; the SLOCC classes identified with the particle kinds; and
monogamy of entanglement identified with confinement. Each theorem was already in the
literature; the claim of this paper is that they are about one another, and the test of that
claim is the record it produces: a Standard Model with no fitted dimensionless parameters (one,
the Koide phase, matched rather than derived), the electroweak vacuum value $v$ its single
dimensional input, and a list of numbered problems where the chain is not yet closed. This
section collects the results by their epistemic stratum, because the paper has insisted
throughout that the strata not be mixed.

\subsection{The chain, in one pass}

Self-reference forces the first structure: a system rich enough to describe itself admits the
inside/outside exchange, Lawvere's theorem applies, and the price of consistency is a number system
in which the parts of a probability can cancel --- complex amplitudes, with the phase as continued
information (\S\ref{sec:self-reference}). Reversible continuity, itself derived conditionally from
the same source (\S\ref{sec:continuity}, Open Problem~\ref{op:limit-realisability}), selects the
$L^{2}$ metric twice over --- by isometry classification and by Hardy's counting
(\S\ref{sec:l2-metric-fibre}) --- and the Born rule arrives by two independent routes, record-theoretic
and geometric (\S\ref{sec:born-rule}, \S\ref{sec:born-invariance}). Wigner's friend becomes an
exercise in the resulting geometry rather than a paradox (\S\ref{sec:wigners-friend}); the
postulates of quantum mechanics assemble from the pieces (\S\ref{sec:postulates}); and the boundary
the whole account keeps invoking --- the classical copy --- is formalised, with its two regimes'
signatures proved synchronised (\S\ref{sec:fanout-boundary},
Proposition~\ref{prop:fanout-correspondence}). The question ``why stop at $\mathbb{C}$?'' then opens
the second act (\S\ref{sec:division-algebras}): the Cayley--Dickson ladder climbs to the octonions
and provably no further, the first spending of that terminal fact excludes $S^{7}$ as a ruler and
fixes three spatial dimensions (\S\ref{sec:space-of-computations}), and the Hopf tower carries the
algebra into geometry, terminating in the stabiliser computation that yields
$G_{\mathrm{SM}} = (SU(3) \times SU(2) \times U(1))/\mathbb{Z}_{6}$
(\S\ref{sec:hopf-justification}, Proposition~\ref{prop:gsm}). The entanglement dictionary then
does the particle physics: product, Bell, GHZ and W classes as Higgs, gauge boson, quark and
lepton (\S\ref{sec:entanglement-classes}), the three-qubit register as the arena
(\S\ref{sec:register}), charges from its geometry (\S\ref{sec:charges}), three generations and one
a-chiral sterile class from the $K_3$ obstruction (\S\ref{sec:generations}), confinement from the
entanglement budget (\S\ref{sec:confinement}), masses from associator debt and phase dilution
(\S\ref{sec:masses}, \S\ref{sec:boson-masses}), the mass mechanism's condensed-matter
witness exhibited (\S\ref{sec:superconductor-witness}), interactions from the gate dictionary
(\S\ref{sec:interactions}), and the predictions, postdictions and kill-conditions collected where
they can be shot at (\S\ref{sec:predictions}).

\subsection{Theorems}

The following are proved, in the text or the appendices, at theorem grade. The inside/outside
fixed-point proposition (Proposition~\ref{prop:inside-outside}); the discreteness and
divisible-closed propositions that price the continuity derivation; the fibre-privacy dictionary at
one qubit (Proposition~\ref{prop:f1-dictionary}) and its register-level extension
(Proposition~\ref{prop:trace-blind}), which together make the global phase's absence from every
classical record a theorem rather than a gloss; the \textsc{fanout} correspondence
(Proposition~\ref{prop:fanout-correspondence}), whose ingredients are the literature's and whose
identification is this paper's; the termination of the Hopf tower
(Proposition~\ref{prop:termination}) and the gauge-group computation (Proposition~\ref{prop:gsm});
the canonical-orbit results --- GHZ confined to the colour triplet with no CP-violating phase, so
that $\theta_{\mathrm{QCD}} = 0$ structurally (Proposition~\ref{prop:ghz}), the W orbit's
$\mathbb{C}$-access ceiling of exactly one ninth (Proposition~\ref{prop:w-access}), and the
$(\tfrac23, \tfrac13)$ marginal that singles out weak isospin (Proposition~\ref{prop:w-doublet}) ---
with their proofs in Appendix~\ref{app:ghz-w-proofs}; the two-ninths identity, by which the access
ceiling and the entanglement-budget slack are one invariant, $r^{2}$, so that confinement and
vanishing singlet-access are one fact (\S\ref{sec:entanglement-classes},
\S\ref{sec:budget-converse}); the absence of an invariant energy locus for binding
(Proposition~\ref{prop:energy-locus}); the absence of tree-level flavour-changing neutral currents
(Proposition~\ref{prop:fcnc}) and the generation-blindness of the neutral gates
(Proposition~\ref{prop:neutral-blind}); and the generation combinatorics of $K_3$, rigorous
conditional on the tripartite extension recorded as Open Problem~\ref{op:filatov-extension}.

\subsection{Structural results, flagged as such}

A second stratum carries the framework's readings: results that organise the theorems into physics
and are flagged, everywhere they appear, as structural rather than proved. The identification of
the selected complex direction with the record direction, delivered in candidate form
(\S\ref{sec:fanout-direction}) with its uniqueness priced (Open Problem~\ref{op:fanout-selection});
the geometric face of the Born rule; measurement as cone-tipping and the Unruh reading that
accompanies it; the computational metric and the $(3+1)$ count, numbered as
Open Problems~\ref{op:comp-metric} and~\ref{op:three-dimensions}; the dictionary assignment itself,
supported four independent ways --- tangle structure, Frobenius classification, the octonionic
Hopf projection, and the superconductor witness of \S\ref{sec:superconductor-witness}, where
the Bell and product rows appear as the two strata of the Anderson--Higgs mechanism --- precisely so that no reviewer need take it as stipulation; the frame as the
carrier of the quark's electroweak identity, with the lepton state-certified and the quark
specification-certified (\S\ref{sec:frame}); the forced architecture of the weak vertex; mass as
debt against the selection rather than residence at a rung; and the confinement account whose
strongest objection is stated and answered in its own subsection
(\S\ref{sec:bell-not-counterexample}): the budget alone does not confine; the budget, the sector,
and the access do.

\subsection{The quantitative record}

The numbers, at their honest weights. Postdictive, with no fitted dimensionless parameters and
$v$ the single dimensional input: $\sin^{2}\theta_{W} = \tfrac14$ at tree level, running to
$0.2312$ against the measured $0.23122$; $m_{H} = v/2$ within $1.7\%$; $y_{t} = 1$ within
$0.9\%$; the Koide relation at the matched phase $\delta_{0} = \tfrac29$ to a hundredth of a per
cent; $\theta_{\mathrm{QCD}} = 0$ exactly; eight of the nine charged-fermion masses within about
one per cent, the ninth fenced as Open Problem~\ref{op:up-quark}. Predictive, and exposed: the
massless lightest neutrino, normal ordering, $\sum m_{\nu} \approx 52$~meV, two sterile states
at the matching scale near $3.6$~TeV, the heavy splitting within a few per cent and the light
splitting honestly wrong by a factor of thirty (Open Problem~\ref{op:neutrino-splitting}) ---
with JUNO's ordering measurement named as the cleanest kill. Null, forever: no flavour-changing
neutral currents above the charged-loop floor, exactly Standard-Model Higgs couplings, no fourth
generation, no new physics below the matching scale, no unification-mediated proton decay. And
instantiated: the framework's structural claims run as circuits on quantum hardware --- the
hadron skeletons assembled from their wiring, the charged-current mixing returning $\lvert
V_{ud}\rvert$ from the framework's phase --- so that ``testable'' has already begun to mean
``tested by construction'' --- most recently the witness section's Meissner response, sign flip
measured on a Heron-class device (Appendix~\ref{app:hardware-fez}) (\S\ref{sec:predictions}).

\subsection{The open-problem economy}

The paper's honesty is architectural: every gap is a numbered problem, listed in the index that
follows this summary, and the statuses --- proved, structural, owed, open --- are never silently
upgraded. The economy is not decorative, and the paper itself is the evidence: several of its own
problems were partly paid during its writing. The scheme-consistent account of the $W/Z$ ratio gap
dissolved into a running-and-dictionary statement; the complex clause of the Hopf--Frobenius
conjecture was discharged by the fibre-privacy dictionary; the leptonic ninth acquired its
geometric identity, leaving only its operational half open. The flagship problems that remain ---
the two premises of continuity (Open Problem~\ref{op:limit-realisability}), the functor of the
inside/outside exchange (Open Problem~\ref{op:sigma-functor}), the compositional conjecture
(Open Problem~\ref{op:hopf-frobenius}), the uniqueness of the selection
(Open Problem~\ref{op:fanout-selection}), the map from network geodesics to physical mass
(Open Problem~\ref{op:associator-geodesic}), and the quartic coupling
(Open Problem~\ref{op:higgs-lambda}) --- are stated with candidate routes and, where possible,
with the constraints any solution must meet. They are written as invitations. A framework that
derives this much from this little should expect, and welcome, many hands.

\subsection{What remains, and where it goes}
\label{sec:what-remains}

Every deferral in this paper has a destination, and we collect them so the reader can see the
programme whole. The dynamical layer --- propagators, rates, the kinetic terms, the completion of
the all-orders flavour statement, and the unification of quantum mechanics with general relativity
at ordinary scales through \textsc{fanout} as causal dressing --- is the second paper. The
cosmological consequences, with the Landauer accounting of the information-theoretic foundations,
are the third. Dark matter as relational solitons at galactic scale is a fourth, contingent on the
second and third. Five shorter companions branch from specific sections: the confinement potential
and the running of the strong coupling, where the budget-commitment reading meets the
renormalisation group; the fourth-qubit programme, in which the sedenion tag carries the
generations and the CKM and PMNS matrices are the operational shadow of its zero divisors
(\S\ref{sec:interactions}); exotic hadrons as network topology, the taxonomy this paper opened and
did not walk (\S\ref{sec:exotic-taxonomy}); the Anderson--Higgs dictionary, developing the
superconductor witness of \S\ref{sec:superconductor-witness} --- the entanglement-stiffness
route to the boson masses and the thin-spectrum model of vacuum selection --- into a study of its
own; and the tripartite extension of the Filatov
correspondence, recorded as Open Problem~\ref{op:filatov-extension} and reserved, with its author's
knowledge, for the colleague whose two-qubit result it extends. The mass gap is named and declined
where it belongs (\S\ref{sec:mass-gap}); the adelic constraints on the mass spectrum and the
categorical completion of the boundary functor stand in the problem index for whoever moves first.
The paper's claims end here; its programme does not.
