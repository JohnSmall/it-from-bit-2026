\section{Sedenions and the Three Generations}
\label{sec:sedenions-generations}

The Standard Model repeats itself three times. Three generations of fermions carry identical gauge
quantum numbers and differ only in mass and mixing, and the repetition is among the flattest of the
model's unexplained facts: nothing in the gauge structure asks for it, and nothing forbids a fourth.
The register of \S\ref{sec:register} gives a first account of the number --- the frustrated-pair
combinatorics of \S\ref{sec:generations} count three plus a sterile class --- but that account, as
noted where it was given, imports a distinction it does not derive: it must know which qubit is the
odd one out and what Cayley--Dickson level it occupies, while the canonical orbits of
\S\ref{sec:canonical-orbits} treat the three qubits as interchangeable. The count is right; the
mechanism was owed. This section pays it, by taking one step further up the Cayley--Dickson ladder,
and in doing so joins a line of work --- pursued independently and in more developed form by
Gresnigt and collaborators, and drawn on by Szangolies --- that reaches the same three from the
same algebra. The connection is established enough to belong in the main text rather than a sequel.

What the framework adds to that line is a reason and a boundary. The reason is why the fourth rung
supplies a family label and never a fourth force; the boundary is why the tower stops there, with
forces spent at three wires and flavour appearing at the fourth. Both fall out of a single algebraic
fact about what the Cayley--Dickson doubling does to the automorphism group, set against the
termination theorems already in hand (Proposition~\ref{prop:termination}).

\subsection{The rough edge, and where the index really lives}
\label{sec:sedenions-roughedge}

The register-level count of \S\ref{sec:generations} enumerated the eight handedness assignments of
the three-qubit frustrated pair and found four classes: three in which exactly one qubit's
handedness is the odd one out, its Cayley--Dickson level $\mathbb{C}$, $\mathbb{H}$ or $\mathbb{O}$
labelling the generation, and one all-same class with no frustrated pair, read as the sterile
sector. The construction works and reproduces the correct doublet structure. But it carries a seam.
The canonical orbits that define a particle (\S\ref{sec:canonical-orbits}) are permutation-symmetric
in the three qubits --- the state does not distinguish them --- whereas the generation reading needs
to know which qubit is odd and, worse, needs that qubit's rung identity, a piece of the tower's
scaffolding rather than a property of the state. Nothing is inconsistent; a label is being read off
the architecture that the state itself does not carry.

The diagnosis came from an unexpected quarter, and it is worth recording because it is the paper's
method visible at work. Extending the executed Cabibbo circuit (Appendix~\ref{app:qiskit-ckm}) to
the full three-generation mixing matrix (Appendix~\ref{app:hw-ckm}) could not be done on the
three-qubit register at all: the construction demanded a fourth qubit to carry the generation index.
Read first as an embarrassment --- an unlicensed wire with no rung --- it is in fact the resolution.
Four qubits are exactly the complexified sedenions,
\begin{equation}
  (\mathbb{C}^{2})^{\otimes 4} \;=\; \mathbb{C}^{16} \;=\; \mathbb{C}\otimes\mathbb{S},
  \label{eq:four-qubit-sedenion}
\end{equation}
one Cayley--Dickson rung past the three-qubit register $\mathbb{C}^{8} = \mathbb{C}\otimes\mathbb{O}$
that the whole construction has inhabited since \S\ref{sec:register}. The generation index does not
live on the register. It lives on the doubling, and the register could not hold it because the
symmetry that moves it is not present one rung down --- which is exactly why the frustrated-pair
reading had to reconstruct the index combinatorially, out of rung labels it was not entitled to. In
the chain of observers of \S\ref{sec:wigners-friend} the fourth wire is the observer of Wigner, and
\S\ref{sec:wf-chain-type} records what his arrival adds and what the arrival of every later observer
does not.

\subsection{The triality promotion}
\label{sec:sedenions-promotion}

The algebraic fact that changes the symmetry inventory is a single line, and it is the pivot of the
whole section. The octonions' automorphism group is connected: $\mathrm{Aut}(\mathbb{O}) = G_2$ has
no component group, and the triality of $\mathrm{Spin}(8)$ --- the outer $S_3$ that permutes the
vector and two spinor representations, the ``deeper reason'' behind the register count of
\S\ref{sec:generations} --- is not induced by any automorphism of the octonions themselves. Doubling
to the sedenions changes exactly that. Brown's theorem gives
\begin{equation}
  \mathrm{Aut}(\mathbb{S}) \;\cong\; G_2 \times S_3,
  \label{eq:brown}
\end{equation}
a direct product: the automorphism group of the sedenions is the automorphism group of the
octonions times exactly one finite factor, and the finite factor commutes with all of $G_2$
\autocite{brown1967generalized}. The factor can be written down. On
$\mathbb{S} = \mathbb{O}\oplus\mathbb{O}u$, with $u$ the doubling unit, it is generated by the
doubling involution $\varepsilon(a + bu) = a - bu$ and by the order-three map
\begin{equation}
  \psi(a + bu) \;=\; \tfrac14\bigl[a + 3\bar a + \sqrt3\,(b - \bar b)\bigr]
              \;+\; \tfrac14\bigl[b + 3\bar b - \sqrt3\,(a - \bar a)\bigr]\,u ,
  \label{eq:brown-psi}
\end{equation}
which fixes $1$ and $u$ and rotates every plane $(a,\,au)$, $a$ an imaginary octonion, by a third
of a turn. The map is the Cayley--Dickson step turning triality from an outer, representation-level
symmetry into a manifest automorphism, and its orbit is the object the generations live on: $\psi$
permutes cyclically the three octonion subalgebras $\mathbb{O}$, $\psi\mathbb{O}$,
$\psi^{2}\mathbb{O}$ --- three ways of writing $\mathbb{S}$ as an octonion doubled by the one unit
$u$, meeting only in the real line, the three involutions of the $S_3$ being the three doubling
involutions. The generation index is the wire on which this permutation acts; the register could
not carry it because the permutation does not act one rung down.

On this reading the three generations are the $S_3$-orbit of a single sector --- the second and
third generations are $\psi$ and $\psi^{2}$ of the first --- and the number three is the order of
$\psi$, a theorem of the algebra rather than the output of a frustration count. Three structural
consequences follow at once, and each repairs something the register account left unexplained.
Because the product in~\eqref{eq:brown} is direct, $\psi$ commutes with the whole of $G_2$ and hence
intertwines every gauge action built from it: whatever gauge quantum numbers the first sector
carries, its two images carry identically. Generation is gauge-blind by construction, and this is
the framework's reason for the untriplicated gauge sector that Gresnigt's construction exhibits by
explicit commutators (\S\ref{sec:sedenions-literature}). The sterile class is the $S_3$-invariant
sector --- the fixed line of $\psi$, spanned by the real axis and the doubling unit, with nothing
to permute --- matching its lack of a frustrated pair in the register picture without any separate
story; we carry that identification at candidate status. And the fourth qubit stops requiring
apology: it is not an unlicensed wire but the carrier of a symmetry that genuinely lives at this
rung and nowhere below.

A fourth consequence answers the objection an exact family symmetry always invites: an exact
automorphism relating the three generations seems to force three equal masses, and the observed
hierarchy seems to demand that the symmetry be broken. Neither follows. An exact symmetry of the
algebra forces degeneracy only in observables invariant under it, and mass is not one. Mass is
associator debt read against the selected complex direction (\S\ref{sec:masses}), and $\psi$ does
not fix that direction: its fixed set is exactly the line spanned by $1$ and the doubling unit
(Appendix~\ref{app:notebook-record}), every other direction being turned a third of a turn into
its doubled partner. The three generations are therefore three exact copies of one structure held
at three orientations to the frame, and the debt each owes is orientation-dependent --- three
orientations, three masses, nothing broken. The mass functional is $S_3$-equivariant rather than
invariant, applying $\psi$ permuting the masses without changing the set, and the symmetry's
exact content survives precisely where the mass formula is exact: in the count of three, in the
$2\pi/3$ spacing of the phases in~\eqref{eq:z3}, and hence in the Koide ratio, which
Proposition~\ref{prop:koide} derives from the doubling norm alone, independently of the phase
$\delta$. On this reading the Koide relation \emph{is} the unbroken $S_3$ as seen in the mass
sector --- the invariant of the orbit --- while the hierarchy is the frame datum, the offset
$\delta$ at which the selected direction sits against the orbit, an offset that reappears,
suggestively, as the first mixing angle (\S\ref{sec:masses}). We carry the reading at candidate
status. It has one immediate merit: the pattern the generations actually present --- identical in
every gauge quantum number, different only in mass and mixing, the two frame-referenced quantities
--- is the signature this account predicts, and the one a broken symmetry would not, since
breaking would generically spoil the $2/3$.

\begin{remark}[Two triples, and which one the doubling permutes]
\label{rem:two-triples}
The sedenions contain a second, better-known triple of octonion subalgebras, and the two must not
be confused. Selecting an imaginary direction $e$ of $\mathbb{O}$ and doubling by $u$ splits
$\mathbb{S}$ into three octonion copies $\mathbb{H}\oplus V_i$ sharing the quaternionic core
$\mathbb{H} = \langle 1, e, u, eu\rangle$, where the $V_i$ are the three complex lines of the
$\mathbb{C}^{3}$ orthogonal to $e$, each together with its $u$-partner; this is the split of Gillard
and Gresnigt's first construction \autocite{gillardgresnigt2019three}, which housed one generation
in each copy. Direct computation on all basis products (notebook T,
Appendix~\ref{app:notebook-record}) shows that Brown's $S_3$ stabilises each of these three copies
and that the automorphism cycling them is a lifted element of $G_2$ --- the three-cycle of colour
lines inside the $SU(3)$ that stabilises $e$, fixing the core pointwise. The core triple is
therefore a colour-basis structure one rung up, the exact analogue of the three Fano lines through
the preferred direction at the register, which are the three colour lines of $\mathbb{C}^{3}$ and
not three generations. A construction that houses one generation per copy accordingly triplicates
the colour algebra, one $SU(3)$ per copy; the later papers of the same line generate the
generations as the $\psi$-orbit of a single sector instead
\autocite{gresnigtgourlayvarma2023three,gresnigt2026three}, and the direct product
in~\eqref{eq:brown} is why that removes the triplication. This paper adopts the $\psi$-orbit. An
earlier draft of this section, and the structural scan recorded as notebook M, worked with the core
triple; both are re-pointed, and the route is logged.
\end{remark}

\subsection{Flavour, not force: the reason the doubling adds a label}
\label{sec:sedenions-flavour-not-force}

The promotion~\eqref{eq:brown} settles, with a proof-shaped reason, a question the register left as
an observation: why the fourth rung supplies a family label and never a fourth interaction. The
reason is a separation the tower makes for us, between the identity component of an automorphism
group and its component group.

Every force in this framework is carried by a connected Lie group acting on a Hopf fibration. The
gauge group is the stabiliser of the selected complex direction inside $\mathrm{Spin}(9)$
(Proposition~\ref{prop:gsm}), with colour the part of $G_2 = \mathrm{Aut}(\mathbb{O})$ that survives
the selection, and the fibrations that host these actions stop at the octonions
(Proposition~\ref{prop:termination}). Now compare the two ends of the doubling. Passing from
$\mathbb{O}$ to $\mathbb{S}$ leaves the identity component of the automorphism group exactly where it
was,
\begin{equation}
  \mathrm{Aut}(\mathbb{S})_0 \;=\; G_2 \;=\; \mathrm{Aut}(\mathbb{O}),
  \label{eq:identity-component}
\end{equation}
and adds precisely the finite factor $S_3$. A finite factor is the component group $\pi_0$; it has no
Lie algebra; and an object with no Lie algebra contributes no generators to any connection form.
Whatever the fourth wire carries is therefore a discrete label and never a coupling charge: there is
no continuous group for it to gauge, and --- since the doubling also destroys the fibration, the
sedenions having zero divisors and admitting no Hopf map $S^{31}\to S^{16}$ --- there is not even a
base manifold on which a connection could live. The conclusion is forced from both sides. Generation
number is conserved and ungauged: flavour, not force.

This is the structural content behind the no-force propositions of \S\ref{sec:flavour-conservation},
and it is worth stating why the two statements are the same. There it is shown that a neutral gate
has no qubit to act on that would carry the generation, so the generation index is inaccessible to
the gauge action; here we see \emph{why} no such qubit's symmetry could ever be gauged, namely that
the symmetry moving the index is a component group. The gauge content of the whole tower lives in the
identity component and terminates at three wires; the doubling to the fourth adds $\pi_0$ and only
$\pi_0$. That is the entire difference between a generation and a colour.

\begin{remark}[The absence of a fifth force is a prediction, not an assumption]
\label{rem:no-fifth-force}
The reading makes the non-appearance of new forces beyond the fourth rung a consequence rather than
an input, and sharpens the termination already argued from the Hopf tower. Forces require a connected
group acting on a fibration; the fibrations end at $\mathbb{O}$ by Adams and the normed division
algebras end there by Hurwitz (Proposition~\ref{prop:termination}); the sedenion doubling adds no
fibration and no Lie-algebra generators, only the finite $S_3$. A fifth Cayley--Dickson rung, the
trigintaduonions, adds exactly one further $S_3$ and nothing else: Brown proved the equality
$\mathrm{Aut}(A_{n-1})\times S_3 = \mathrm{Aut}(A_n)$ for the sedenions and the next two rungs,
Eakin and Sathaye that the pattern continues at every rung, and the derivation algebra is
$\mathfrak{g}_2$ from the octonions onward, so no new connected symmetry ever appears
\autocite{brown1967generalized,eakinsathaye1990automorphisms,schafer1954cayleydickson}; the fifth
and sixth rungs are checked directly in the notebook record (Appendix~\ref{app:notebook-record}).
The literature's expectation that nothing beyond the sedenions brings new physics is here given its
reason, and its precise form: the increment becomes constant. The discovery of a genuine fifth force, or of a fourth
generation on the same footing as the first three, would therefore refute the construction, and this
is among its sharpest falsifiers.
\end{remark}

\subsection{The established line: Gresnigt, and Szangolies's use of it}
\label{sec:sedenions-literature}

The sedenion route to the generation count is not this paper's discovery, and the record should say
so plainly, because the convergence is itself the strongest evidence that the rung is the right one.
That $\mathrm{Aut}(\mathbb{S}) = G_2 \times S_3$ furnishes exactly three copies of the octonion
algebra, and that these can carry three fermion generations, is a line developed over several years by
Gresnigt and collaborators, in more explicit and more complete form than the sketch above. It began
with the complex sedenions realising three generations with two unbroken gauge symmetries
\autocite{gillardgresnigt2019three}, was refined to a colour-symmetric construction in which the
order-three $S_3$ automorphism generates the second and third generations from the first
\autocite{gresnigtgourlayvarma2023three}, and was then extended to include a correctly-assigning
$U(1)$ within the Clifford algebra $\mathbb{C}\ell(8)$ \autocite{gourlaygresnigt2024algebraic}. A
parallel leptonic construction reaches the same three octonion copies inside the sedenions
\autocite{tangtang2023unified}, and the four-qubit-as-$S^{31}$ parametrisation on which the
entanglement reading rests is older still \autocite{pinillaluthra2009hopf}. The essential facts we
lean on --- the promotion of triality to a manifest $S_3$, the generation map $\psi$ that generates
the second and third generations from the first, and the $S_3$-invariance of the gauge sector ---
are theirs; the line's own passage from the core split of 2019 to the $\psi$-orbit is discussed in
Remark~\ref{rem:two-triples}.

The most recent step in that line is also the most directly relevant to the present framework, and it
resolves the one feature the framework most needs a generation mechanism to have. Gresnigt has now
shown that the complex Clifford algebra $\mathbb{C}\ell(10)$ realises three linearly independent
fermion generations, each carrying the correct quantum numbers under the \emph{single},
untriplicated Standard Model gauge algebra
$\mathfrak{su}(3)_C \oplus \mathfrak{su}(2)_L \oplus \mathfrak{u}(1)_Y$, with an embedded $S_3$ family
action that cyclically permutes the three fermion sectors while leaving every gauge generator fixed
\autocite{gresnigt2026three}. The construction is the electroweak completion of the $\mathbb{C}\ell(8)$
work: passing from $\mathbb{C}\ell(8)$ to $\mathbb{C}\ell(10)$ supplies the extra Witt pair needed for
the chiral weak sector and the physical hypercharge, and the $S_3$ is fixed to be
$G_2$-commuting and gauge-preserving, so that the gauge sector is emphatically \emph{not} replicated.
Three points of contact deserve statement.

First, Gresnigt's central structural result --- that the family $S_3$ leaves the gauge generators
invariant, so the gauge sector is untriplicated --- is, in the vocabulary of this paper, the
statement that the generation symmetry is a discrete label carried by the fermion sectors and not a
gauge symmetry of the connection. His construction proves it at the level of explicit Clifford
generators and commutators: the colour and weak generators are $S_3$-fixed, $\psi_3(\Lambda_i) =
\Lambda_i$ and $\psi_3(T_i) = T_i$, and the family action moves only the fermion sectors. The present
framework supplies the same conclusion's reason from one rung up: the $S_3$ is the component group of
$\mathrm{Aut}(\mathbb{S})$, which by~\eqref{eq:identity-component} has no Lie algebra to gauge, and
which commutes with the identity component, so that the images of a sector under the family action
are gauge-equivalent before any generator is written down. The
two accounts are complementary in exactly the way the framework's relation to the algebraic programmes
usually is (\S\ref{sec:entanglement-szangolies}): Gresnigt fixes the representation content and proves
the non-replication by construction; the framework says why non-replication was forced --- a component
group cannot carry a force. Notably, Gresnigt is careful that his $\psi_3$ is \emph{not} standard
$\mathrm{Spin}(8)$ triality but the sedenion-induced $S_3$, and leaves the precise relation between
the two as an open question; this is the same distinction the register account met as ``triality
gives three, the $K_3$ device refines it,'' and the same open relation appears here as the shadow
theorem below (\S\ref{sec:sedenions-shadow}).

Second, Szangolies --- whose $\mathrm{Spin}(9)$ construction this paper already leans on
(\S\ref{sec:entanglement-szangolies}) --- reaches for exactly this rung in closing his own tower. His
final development names the sedenions as the tempting next algebra, notes that their zero divisors
forbid a fourth Hopf map so that no fully analogous continuation exists, records that a four-qubit
state can nonetheless be parametrised as a point of $S^{31}$ with the sedenions as scaffolding
\autocite{pinillaluthra2009hopf}, and cites the discrete $S_3$ of $\mathrm{Aut}(\mathbb{S})$ as
realising the three generations as three copies of the octonion algebra
\autocite{gresnigtgourlayvarma2023three,gourlaygresnigt2024algebraic,tangtang2023unified}, with
``no essentially new phenomenology (e.g.\ fifth forces)'' expected from the enlargement. His summary
--- generational structure but no new force --- is precisely the reading this section endorses, and
Remark~\ref{rem:no-fifth-force} is its reason. The two frameworks, arriving at the sedenion rung from
the gauge-representation side and from the entanglement side, agree on what it delivers.

Third, the two frameworks spend the same sixteen complex dimensions differently, and the difference
is worth stating so that it is not mistaken for disagreement. Szangolies reads $\mathbb{C}^{16}$ as
$\mathbb{O}^4$ --- two copies of the octonionic Hopf fibration --- and spends the doubling on
chirality and mass, the second octonionic fibration supplying the right-handed partner and the
massive Dirac structure. This paper reads the same space as $\mathbb{C}\otimes\mathbb{S}$ and spends
the doubling on the generation index, chirality having been housed at the register (the Filatov
handedness of \S\ref{sec:generations}) and mass as associator debt (\S\ref{sec:masses}). These are
two decompositions of one Hilbert space, selected for different work, not rival claims about it;
their reconciliation --- whether the $\mathbb{O}^4$ chirality reading and the
$\mathbb{C}\otimes\mathbb{S}$ generation reading are two projections of a single structure --- is a
natural question, and one we leave open rather than settle here.

\subsection{The register count as a shadow}
\label{sec:sedenions-shadow}

Two accounts of the generation number are now on the table: the register's frustrated-pair count of
\S\ref{sec:generations}, and the sedenion $S_3$-orbit of \S\ref{sec:sedenions-promotion}. They are
not rivals. The candidate relation, stated plainly as a conjecture, is that the first is the
\emph{shadow} of the second --- the $S_3$ action on the three halvings
$\{\mathbb{O}, \psi\mathbb{O}, \psi^{2}\mathbb{O}\}$, projected through the selected complex
direction down to the three-qubit register, reproducing the frustration classes there. On this
reading the register account is not wrong but incomplete: it sees, in the odd-qubit-out
combinatorics, the register-level image of a permutation whose home is one rung up, which is exactly
why it had to borrow rung labels to name what it saw.

Two honesty notes govern how much of this is secured. The abstract half is automatic and carries no
weight: an $S_3$ acting on any three-element set is $\mathrm{Aut}(\mathbb{Z}_2\times\mathbb{Z}_2)$
acting on the non-identity elements, so the fact that the four $K_3$ classes form a Klein four-group
under class addition, with $S_3$ as its automorphism group, matches the halvings by group theory
alone. And the candidate bridge offered in earlier drafts --- the three Fano lines through the
preferred direction matched to the three octonion copies through the quaternionic core --- is
withdrawn: both sides of it are colour-basis objects (Remark~\ref{rem:two-triples}), so it is a true
statement about colour and silent about generations. What remains is the physical intertwiner, and
on present evidence neither outcome is favoured: the register classes distinguish which rung is odd,
the halvings distinguish the orientation of the completed link against its copy, and no map between
the two has yet been proposed. We state the problem in that form.

\begin{openproblem}[The shadow theorem]
\label{op:generation-mechanism}
Show that the $S_3$ action on the three halvings
$\{\mathbb{O}, \psi\mathbb{O}, \psi^{2}\mathbb{O}\}$ of $\mathbb{S}$ (the map~\eqref{eq:brown-psi}),
projected through the selected complex direction, reproduces the $K_3$ frustration classes on the
three-qubit register: the three odd-qubit-out classes as the images of the three halvings, the
all-same class as the $\psi$-invariant sector. \textup{PASS}: an explicit selected-direction
projection carrying halving $k$ to frustration class $k$, $S_3$-equivariantly. \textup{FAIL}: no
such projection exists, in which case the two counts of three are numerically coincident but
structurally distinct, the $\psi$-orbit is the mechanism of record and the register count a device
--- itself an informative outcome, and the same open relation Gresnigt records between his sedenion
$S_3$ and standard $\mathrm{Spin}(8)$ triality \autocite{gresnigt2026three}. The Fano-line bridge is
recorded as tried and withdrawn (colour on both sides) so that it is not retried.
\end{openproblem}

\subsection{Mixing, and what is deferred}
\label{sec:sedenions-mixing}

The sedenion reading offers a handle on quark and lepton mixing, and honesty requires separating what
it delivers now from what it only promises. Two structural facts are in hand. First, the fourth-party
generation tag can never be perfectly correlated with the three-qubit register, because no four-qubit
state is maximally entangled across every bipartition \autocite{higuchisudbery2000,hubersiewert2026};
generations cannot superselect, so nontrivial mixing is obligatory rather than optional. Second, the
same zero divisors that forbid the fourth fibration are seated where the generation map acts: the
sedenions' zero divisors lie on the diagonals between the link and its copy, the very planes
$(a, au)$ that $\psi$ rotates, so the failure of division and the family rotation inhabit one sector
and generation number is a conserved label except where that sector is crossed. The Cabibbo rung is
already executed on hardware, with the mixing angle recovered as $\lambda = \sin(2/9)$ to the
per-cent level (Appendix~\ref{app:qiskit-ckm}). A structural scan of the bare cross-copy vertex
found it maximally mixing, which would put the observed near-diagonality of the mixing matrices
entirely in the dressing; that scan was run on the core triple of Remark~\ref{rem:two-triples} and
must be re-run on the $\psi$-orbit before its verdict is carried over to generations --- an owed
computation, recorded as such.

A further recognition ties the count to the paper's foundations, and we state it with its
status decomposed. Three is not only the order of the $S_3$-orbit; it is exactly the threshold
at which a mixing matrix acquires an irremovable complex phase --- Kobayashi and Maskawa's
counting gives $(N-1)(N-2)/2$ physical phases, zero at two generations and one at three
\autocite{kobayashimaskawa1973} --- and the invariant form of the irremovability is Jarlskog's
$J$ \autocite{jarlskog1985}. Writing $V_{ij} = \braket{u_i|d_j}$ for the overlap of the up- and
down-type mass eigenbases, the rephasing-invariant quartets are fourth-order Bargmann invariants
alternating between the two bases,
\begin{equation}
  V_{ij}V_{kl}V^{*}_{il}V^{*}_{kj}
  \;=\; \braket{u_i|d_j}\braket{d_j|u_k}\braket{u_k|d_l}\braket{d_l|u_i},
  \label{eq:jarlskog-bargmann}
\end{equation}
and $J$ is their common imaginary part. $J \neq 0$ therefore says that no phase convention makes
every element real at once: each element can be realified locally, the collection cannot be
realified globally, and this local-versus-global obstruction is precisely the shape
\S\ref{sec:contextuality} identifies as contextuality --- there proved equivalent to negativity
\autocite{spekkens2008negativity} in the signed calculus from which this paper's amplitudes were
derived in the first place (\S\ref{sec:self-reference}); nonreal invariants of exactly this
Bargmann form are likewise the currency of the Kirkwood--Dirac literature already drawn on there
\autocite{arvidssonshukur2024kirkwood}. Within the framework, then, ``irremovable phase implies
contextual data'' is a corollary of a chain already run, not an imported association.

Where the data live is the reading this section supplies, at candidate status. One added wire,
three generations, one phase: the uniqueness of the phase matches the uniqueness of the
doubling, and the fourth wire --- the observer of Wigner (\S\ref{sec:wf-chain-type}) --- is the
anchor from which the flavour phase is physical at all, the flavour sector's Wigner's-friend
phase, real only from one rung above. The executed record is consonant: the two-generation
Cabibbo circuit is a \emph{real} rotation, and at $N = 2$ the counting licenses exactly zero
phases, so the real circuit was not a simplification but the whole content; the third
generation, where a phase first becomes irremovable, is exactly where the construction demanded
its additional wire. At the same candidate status, and without touching the standing structural
derivation of $\theta_{\mathrm{QCD}} = 0$ (\S\ref{sec:confinement}), the reading offers one
sentence for two facts: CP violation lives in the tag sector and only there --- a contextual
phase where there is a context-bearing wire, $\delta_{\mathrm{CKM}} \neq 0$, and none on the
connection side, which has no such wire to offer, $\theta_{\mathrm{QCD}} = 0$.

What this does not yet deliver is the full CKM and PMNS phenomenology: the mass hierarchy, the
individual mixing angles, the value of the CP phase, and the striking asymmetry between large
lepton mixing and small hierarchical quark mixing. These are the subject of a dedicated follow-up, where the four-qubit
grading is treated as the arena --- the physical mixing data as moduli of the four-wire entanglement
structure, with the generation $S_3$ acting through triality. The Higgs-sector and Yukawa-texture
questions that must accompany any such account are, in the algebraic line, exactly where Gresnigt's
programme is now headed \autocite{gourlaygresnigt2024algebraic,gresnigt2026three}, and the convergence
of the two routes on that frontier is itself encouragement. This paper claims the backbone --- three
generations from the sedenion doubling, the family symmetry a discrete label and not a force, the
count fixed at three and the tower closed --- and defers the quantitative flavour phenomenology
rather than overreaching for it here.

\subsection{Summary}
\label{sec:sedenions-summary}

The three generations are the $S_3$-orbit of a single sector under the sedenions' order-three
automorphism, a rung past the register the rest of this paper inhabits. The count is three because
Brown's theorem makes $\mathrm{Aut}(\mathbb{S}) = G_2 \times S_3$ and the orbit has that order; the
generations share their gauge quantum numbers because the product is direct, so the family map
commutes with every gauge action; the sterile sector is the $S_3$-invariant piece. This is an established line --- Gresnigt and collaborators have
developed it in explicit Clifford-algebraic form, most recently realising three untriplicated
generations inside $\mathbb{C}\ell(10)$, and Szangolies draws on it in the same spirit as this paper.
The framework's own contribution is narrow and specific: not the count, which is the literature's, but
the reason the doubling adds a label rather than a force --- the added factor is the component group
$\pi_0$, which carries no Lie algebra and gauges nothing --- and the placement of that reason inside a
tower whose forces already terminate at three wires by Hurwitz, Adams and Coecke--Kissinger. Forces at
three, flavour at four, and closed thereafter.
