\section{Hardware Results on a Heron-Class Device}
\label{app:hardware-fez}

The computations of the preceding appendices established the framework's circuits on simulators
and, where noted, on hardware. This appendix records a five-notebook batch executed on
\texttt{ibm\_fez}, a Heron-class processor, on 8--11 July 2026 at $8192$ shots per setting
($16384$ for the mitigated repetition of the ninth), with job identifiers retained in the code
release. The batch tests five distinct claims from five sections of the paper; all five are
confirmed. The first pass left two quantitative refinements owed and priced; both were designed
with criteria pre-registered before submission, executed on 11 July, and passed every gate ---
they are reported in their subsections below, with the first-pass values retained beside them.
First-pass values are reported unmitigated, with each run's dominant noise mechanism read
explicitly --- the readings are part of the result, since a confirmation whose noise behaves
wrongly would be no confirmation at all; the two repetitions report their correction ladders step
by step, so no corrected number is quoted without its raw partner.

\subsection{The monogamy signature}
\label{app:hw-monogamy}

Tomography of the three-qubit GHZ register returns a collective tangle
$\tau_{1(23)} = 1.000$ with pairwise concurrences $C_{12} = C_{13} = 0.000$ and residual
externally available tangle zero: everything in the collective, nothing pairwise --- the
entanglement-budget signature of \S\ref{sec:confinement}, on hardware. One honesty clause is owed
and paid here: this particular signature is confirmed with noise-\emph{robustness} rather than
despite noise, because the maximally mixed marginal is a fixed point of depolarisation --- the
hardware is pulled \emph{toward} $\tau = 1$ --- and the Wootters floor pins separable pair
marginals at zero concurrence. The signature is real; its hardware cleanliness is partly the
noise's gift, and a reader auditing the tomography should know so.

\subsection{The two-gluon network's open legs}
\label{app:hw-two-gluon}

The two-gluon network, postselected at $2089/8192 = 0.255$ against the ideal $\tfrac14$, shows
open-leg populations $0.504$ and $0.438$ on the Bell diagonal with $5.8\%$ leakage --- and the
number that decides the question: $\langle XX \rangle = +0.915$ against the ideal $+1$. A
classical copy tensor at the open legs would reproduce the same diagonal with
$\langle XX \rangle \approx 0$; the measured coherence is therefore the discriminator firing as
designed. The open legs of the two-gluon network are a Bell pair, not a \textsc{fanout} --- the
distinction the glueball construction of \S\ref{sec:confinement} requires, measured.

\subsection{The leptonic ninth}
\label{app:hw-ninth}

Single-wire tomography of the canonical W register with a basis-copying tag returns
$r^{2} = 0.0935$ against the target $\tfrac19 = 0.1111$, and pair tomography returns
$C(\text{wire}, \text{tag}) = 0.0162$ against the target zero. The deficit in $r^{2}$ is sixteen
per cent \emph{low}, which is the depolarisation prediction in sign and size --- Bloch vectors
shrink as $(1-p)$ per axis, here $p \approx 0.08$ over circuit depths of $27$--$41$ --- and the
small concurrence is at exactly the scale the Wootters floor manufactures from correlated readout.
The two-ninths identity's operational content (\S\ref{sec:entanglement-classes},
\S\ref{sec:bell-not-counterexample}) therefore stands measured: the ninth is base presence, and
the state-level tag concurrence is zero. The mitigated repetition was executed on 11 July with its criteria pre-registered before
submission: the corrected $r^{2}$ within $0.010$ of $\tfrac19$; the probe-predicted raw
$\langle Z\rangle$ agreeing with the measured value within two standard errors, so that the
depolarisation story would be closed quantitatively rather than asserted; and the mitigated
concurrence below $0.010$. The stack was gate and measurement twirling (the former makes the
stochastic-Pauli model that the first pass's deficit reading assumed into an enforced property of
the run), XY4 dynamical decoupling, an in-job readout calibration inverted per qubit, and --- the
primary correction --- two-point affine probes at matched two-qubit-gate count on the wire's
physical qubit, measuring the channel $\langle s\rangle \mapsto a + b\langle s\rangle$ per Bloch
axis; the affine form matters because the device's $T_{1}$ bias enters as an offset that naive
division by a visibility would misread. At $16384$ shots per setting the within-job ladder ran
$0.1011$ (raw under decoupling and twirling) $\to 0.1036$ (readout-corrected) $\to
0.1095 \pm 0.0051$ (probe-corrected) against $\tfrac19 = 0.1111$, with every pre-registered gate
passed: the corrected value sits $0.0016$ from the ceiling; the probes \emph{predicted} the raw
$\langle Z\rangle$ at $+0.3242$ against the measured $+0.3218$ --- the deficit measured and
removed, not assumed; and the concurrence fell to $0.0043$, four times tighter than the first
pass. The corrected Bloch vector $(-0.0054,\, -0.0001,\, +0.3308)$ states the result plainly: the
ninth is pure $Z$-presence, transverse components consistent with zero, exactly as the canonical W
marginal demands. One comparison is stated and deliberately not priced: the first pass's bare
$0.0935$ against this run's raw $0.1011$ crosses days and calibrations, so the
decoupling-and-twirling layer is confounded with drift; the quoted pair is the within-job one. A
zero-noise extrapolation run alongside as the pre-registered secondary check returned $0.1043$,
consistent and gating nothing.

\subsection{The generation register and the mixing core}
\label{app:hw-ckm}

Why the generation register is two qubits and not a qutrit deserves its explanation before the
numbers, because the framework's refusal here is principled rather than practical. The register
architecture descends the Cayley--Dickson chain, one binary distinction --- one qubit --- per
rung, and dimension three is not on the chain; no doubling produces a three-level system. The
qutrit's own geometry certifies the exclusion independently: its pure states form
$\mathbb{CP}^{2}$, a four-dimensional, measure-zero subset of the seven-dimensional boundary of
its state body, where the qubit's pure states \emph{are} its boundary; that boundary is cubic
($\det\rho = 0$) where the qubit's is the quadratic sphere; orthogonal states sit at the vertices
of a triangle rather than at antipodes; and the total space $S^{5}$ of its phase fibration is not
parallelisable. Each failure traces to the absence of a normed division algebra in dimension
three, by the same theorems of Hurwitz and Adams that terminate the gauge tower --- so a
fundamental three-level register would stand outside every structure this paper runs on: no Hopf
fibration beneath it, no privacy-of-phase route to the Born rule, no Frobenius composition law.
The constraint has in fact been paid once already: colour's three is representation multiplicity,
$SU(3)$ acting on the $\mathbb{C}^{3}$ of $\mathbb{O} \cong \mathbb{C} \oplus \mathbb{C}^{3}$,
never a three-level state space --- the framework refuses the qutrit twice, by two different
mechanisms, under the one constraint.

What the two qubits are instead is the next rung itself. Four qubits are
$(\mathbb{C}^{2})^{\otimes 4} = \mathbb{C}^{16} = \mathbb{C} \otimes \mathbb{S}$, the complex
sedenions, exactly as the three-qubit register is
$\mathbb{C}^{8} = \mathbb{C} \otimes \mathbb{O}$: the tag structure is one Cayley--Dickson
doubling past the register, and Brown's theorem gives the doubling's entire automorphism gain as a
permutation factor, $\mathrm{Aut}(\mathbb{S}) = G_{2} \times S_{3}$, the $S_{3}$ permuting three
intersecting octonion subalgebras that share a quaternionic
core~\autocite{gillardgresnigt2019three} (\S\ref{sec:generations}). Three generations under an
$S_{3}$ do not form an irreducible three --- $S_{3}$ has no three-dimensional irreducible
representation --- but the reducible $1 \oplus 2$, trivial plus standard, and the register
realises exactly that: the $t$ qubit flags singlet against doublet, the $d$ qubit rotates within
the doublet, with $\lvert t_{0} d_{0}\rangle = g_{1}$, $\lvert t_{1} d_{0}\rangle = g_{2}$,
$\lvert t_{1} d_{1}\rangle = g_{3}$. A qutrit would thus be doubly wrong: topologically
unsupported, and representation-theoretically mistaken, treating as irreducible a space the
sedenion $S_{3}$ says is reducible. The epistemic split is the notebook's own: the $1 \oplus 2$
organisation is the sedenion $S_{3}$'s representation content; the particular basis realisation is
a recorded design decision, not a derivation --- a single tag qubit sufficed for the
two-generation Cabibbo rung of Appendix~\ref{app:qiskit-ckm}, and the pair is what $1 \oplus 2$
minimally needs.

The pair has four states and the representation needs three. The fourth,
$\lvert t_{0} d_{1}\rangle$ --- the doublet coordinate raised while the singlet flag is down ---
is syntactically available and semantically empty: the embedded mixing unitary decouples it
exactly (below $10^{-12}$ in the constructed operator), and the device measures its emptiness,
$0.022$ on the first pass and $0.005$ mitigated below. The spectator is the price of housing a
three-dimensional representation in the minimal qubit register, and its measured vacancy is the
circuit-level face of --- though not a proof of --- the algebra's refusal of a fourth generation.
Two clauses complete the register's honest description. Mixing is implemented at \emph{frame}
level, a basis rotation at the vertex rather than a tag-register entangling operation, per the
budget theorem's quark side: the quark register offers exactly zero state-level external
correlation room, where the leptonic ninth of Appendix~\ref{app:hw-ninth} is precisely such room
--- the recorded engine, at structural-candidate status, of the observed CKM-small/PMNS-large
asymmetry. And mixing at this rung is obligatory rather than optional: no four-qubit state is
maximally entangled across every bipartition \autocite{higuchisudbery2000}, so a generation tag
can never correlate perfectly with the register --- generations cannot superselect, and a
nontrivial mixing matrix is the operational shadow of the sedenion zero divisors (conjecture
status, developed in the dedicated follow-up).

The two-qubit generation core of the mixing circuit, at transpiled depths $13$--$15$, returns the
full $|V_{ij}|^{2}$ table with maximum deviation $0.032$ from the programmed pure-power baseline
and spectator leakage $0.022$ (ideal zero --- pure hardware). At the first rung,
$|V_{us}| = 0.233$ measured against the programmed $\sin\tfrac29 = 0.2204$: the Cabibbo
prediction demonstrated on hardware within six per cent, unmitigated. The second and third rungs
are read at $0.0214$ and $0.0094$ against programmed $0.0024$ and $0.0025$: the $\lambda^{4}$
elements sit below the device's $\sim 2\%$ unmitigated error floor, and their excess is the floor,
not physics. The amplified repetition was executed on 11 July with its criteria pre-registered before
submission: the anchor $|V_{us}|$ within two per cent, the primary $|V_{cb}|$ within the ten per
cent just named, the stretch $|V_{ub}|$ within fifteen per cent reported without gating, and the
fitted visibility reported as a health line. Each rung is a rotation on a two-dimensional
generation subspace, so $G(\theta)^{n} = G(n\theta)$ exactly; amplification is by \emph{physical
repetition} of the same compiled block, barriers preventing the optimiser from collapsing the
product. Compiling $G(n\theta)$ directly would be depth-free but would certify only a large
programmed angle; repetition makes the hardware accumulate its own implemented angle, coherent
miscalibration included, which is what honest metrology of the element must price. Sweeping
$n \le 25$ and fitting $p(n) = \gamma^{n}\sin^{2}(n\theta) + (1-\gamma^{n})\,c$, with
$(\gamma, c)$ shared between the two small rungs --- licensed by their identical two-gate
compilations on the same physical pair, and necessary because the $\lambda^{3}$ signal alone sits
below its own depolarised floor --- the device returned $|V_{us}| = 0.2232$ ($1.27\%$ from
$\lambda$, against six per cent unamplified), $|V_{cb}| = 0.0516$ ($6.2\%$ from $\lambda^{2}$,
the element the first pass read nine times high), and $|V_{ub}| = 0.0111$ ($3.6\%$ from
$\lambda^{3}$). The last number deserves its own sentence: the $\lambda^{3}$ element's direct
transfer probability is $\lambda^{6} \approx 1.2\times10^{-4}$, two orders of magnitude below the
device floor and invisible to the first pass by construction; amplification recovered it to a few
per cent. Two readings close the run honestly. The primary deviation, $+6.2\%$ at roughly seven
standard errors of the fit, is not shot noise but a resolved property of the device: the
divide-by-$n$ estimates are flat from $n \ge 3$, the signature of a constant per-block
over-rotation rather than of decoherence drift --- amplification is a coherent-error microscope,
and the residual is now characterised where the first pass could only bound it; the pre-registered
claim, recovery of the programmed element within ten per cent, passes. And the same job carries
its own control: the full-matrix circuit rerun with readout mitigation alone still reads
$|V_{cb}|^{2}$ at $0.0125$ against the programmed $0.0024$ --- floor-limited however corrected ---
while the amplified route lands at six per cent; mitigation cannot buy these elements, and the
amplification does real epistemic work. The mitigated full table's maximum deviation fell from
$0.032$ to $0.015$ and the spectator leakage from $0.022$ to $0.005$.

What these numbers do and do not establish should be stated with the care the criteria were. The
framework derives one number here with no parameter beyond the matched Koide phase, $\lambda =
\sin\tfrac29$, and with it the four first-rung entries at the per-cent level against experiment
(Appendix~\ref{app:qiskit-ckm}); the second and third rungs enter as the pure-power baseline
$s_{23} = \lambda^{2}$, $s_{13} = \lambda^{3}$, a structural candidate whose order-one
Wolfenstein coefficients and CP phase are exactly the content of Open Problem~\ref{op:cabibbo}
--- the baseline's gaps against the measured matrix ($+16\%$ on $|V_{cb}|$, a factor $2.9$ on
$|V_{ub}|$, with $|V_{td}|$'s near-cancellation at zero phase collapsing into the underived
$\delta$) are those owed objects, printed rather than hidden. Two comparisons therefore run
through this subsection and must not be conflated. Hardware against programmed --- the $1.3\%$,
$6.2\%$ and $3.6\%$ above --- establishes that the framework's structure is \emph{executable}
and that a physical device implements it faithfully down to an element of one part in ten
thousand; that is implementation fidelity and metrology, and no quantum processor can promote it
into more. Framework against nature is the comparison with the measured mixing matrix, made in
the body of the paper and in Appendix~\ref{app:qiskit-ckm} against values determined in
accelerator experiments, with no quantum computer involved. The first comparison's value is that
it readies the second: when the dressed-operator programme delivers corrections to the pure
powers, this pipeline tests the corrected elements at the few-per-cent level on request.

\subsection{The Meissner response}
\label{app:hw-meissner}

The pair-condensate notebook of the superconductor witness (\S\ref{sec:superconductor-witness})
prepares $\lvert D^{4}_{2} \rangle$ at transpiled depths $57$--$73$ and measures the long-range
correlator under a phase twist: $\langle \sigma^{+}_{0}\sigma^{-}_{3} \rangle = +0.2864$,
$-0.0167$, $-0.2948$ at twist $0$, $\tfrac{\pi}{2}$ and $\pi$ across the pair, against the ideal
$+\tfrac13$, $0$, $-\tfrac13$. \emph{The sign flip is measured.} The three points are a single
visibility prefactor $V \approx 0.87$ on the ideal $\cos$ response --- the stiffness's functional
form intact, the amplitude attenuated exactly as the depth predicts, the mid-point and the
end-asymmetry both inside shot tolerance. And the identity verified in the same notebook,
$\tau_{1} = 4\,\langle \sigma^{+}\sigma^{-} \rangle\,(N-1)/N$, converts the measured correlator
into a measured one-site tangle of $0.859$ with no further experiment: the device measured one
number wearing both names --- the order parameter and the entanglement measure as a single
measurement, which is the witness section's central consonance executed rather than argued. A
lower-depth preparation (B\"artschi--Eidenbenz) would raise $V$ toward the mid-nineties and is
noted for the figure; the demonstration does not need it.

The batch, in one line: five claims from five sections, five confirmations, and the two priced
refinements delivered against criteria fixed before the device was asked --- the paper's habit of
inviting its own hardware to try to kill it, continued.
