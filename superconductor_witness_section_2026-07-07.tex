\section{The Superconductor as Witness}
\label{sec:superconductor-witness}

Every structure in the mass story just told was derived from the register: the dictionary's
assignment of particle kinds to entanglement classes, the reading of mass as debt against the
selection, the privacy of the gauge phase, the boson masses from fibre geometry. None of it was
built with condensed matter in mind. This section exhibits an independent physical system in which
the same structure is realised, refined over seventy years, and measured in the laboratory --- the
superconductor --- and records the map at the status it deserves: consonance, exhibited rather
than claimed, with one identity from the literature doing quantitative work. The exhibit matters
because the dictionary's genre can look exotic on first meeting; the superconductor shows that
nature already runs the pattern once, in a system where every step can be checked.

The mechanism is the framework's two dictionary rows, stacked. A Cooper pair is a spin-singlet ---
verbatim a Bell state --- and the superconducting ground state is a coherent condensate of such
pairs: the constituents are Bell-class, the collective order parameter is product-class, the most
classical object the theory offers. The Anderson--Higgs mechanism is then the statement that the
electromagnetic gauge field eats the condensate's phase mode and acquires a mass, expelling flux
--- the Meissner effect --- which is the ancestor, in Anderson's hands before the relativistic
generalisation, of the electroweak story
\autocite{anderson1963plasmons, poniatowski2019superconductivity}. Read against
\S\ref{sec:entanglement-classes}: the framework assigns gauge bosons to the Bell class and the
Higgs to the product class, and the superconductor says those two assignments are the two strata
of one mechanism --- the massive gauge field is the one that has married a condensate built of
Bell pairs. What looked like two independent rows of the dictionary is, in the witness system, a
single process seen at its microscopic and collective levels.

The consonance is quantitative where the models are solvable. For $\eta$-pairing states the
concurrence of entanglement between one site and the rest \emph{equals} the off-diagonal
long-range-order correlator in the thermodynamic limit --- entangled if and only if
superconducting --- and the quantum order parameters of the standard pairing models are directly
related to the ground state's entanglement \autocite{fanlloyd2005eta}. The order parameter whose
stiffness gives the gauge boson its mass is, in these cases, an entanglement measure identically.
A framework that reads particle kinds off entanglement classes and mass off a commitment's
rigidity is therefore not proposing a new genre of physical explanation; it is proposing that the
electroweak vacuum belongs to a genre condensed matter has already made rigorous.

The deepest match is fine structure that neither literature was obliged to have. Elitzur's theorem
forbids the spontaneous breaking of a local gauge symmetry; what breaks in a superconductor is
global phase, and the careful account splits the phenomena accordingly: the Meissner effect ---
the mass --- requires no phase-symmetry breaking at all, while the Josephson effect --- relative
phase between two condensates --- does \autocite{vanwezelvandenbrink2007ssb}. This framework drew
the same line before meeting the witness, and for its own reasons. The gauge phase is the private
fibre: Proposition~\ref{prop:f1-dictionary} makes it unrecordable, so there is nothing there to
lock, and Elitzur's theorem is the privacy doctrine in condensed-matter clothing. The boson masses
of \S\ref{sec:boson-masses} were accordingly derived from fibre geometry with no recorded vacuum
phase anywhere in the construction --- mass without phase-breaking, exactly the Meissner side of
the split --- while recoverable \emph{relative} phase, the quaternionic-fibre content of
\S\ref{sec:wf-reconciliation}, is exactly the Josephson side. Two literatures, two independent
derivations, one line drawn in one place. The match also pre-arms an objection: a reader who
protests that a vacuum expectation value ``breaks gauge symmetry'' in violation of Elitzur will
find that this framework never recorded the gauge phase to begin with, in company with the modern
superconductivity literature.

The debt reading of \S\ref{sec:masses} has its London form. In a superconductor the photon's
mass-squared is the superfluid phase stiffness, and the energy cost falls on whatever twists the
committed phase --- the Meissner effect is the debt collected spatially, gradients priced out of
the bulk. ``Mass is owed by whatever action moves the selection'' is the same sentence in the
other vocabulary, and the kinship extends to the selection event itself: the symmetry-broken
condensate carries a thin spectrum of in-gap states whose analysis makes the choosing of the
broken vacuum a calculable decoherence process \autocite{vanwezelvandenbrink2007ssb} --- which is
to say, a solvable model system for the register-level selection problem this paper prices as
Open Problem~\ref{op:fanout-selection}, noted there as a candidate route.

Three prospects are recorded and deliberately left to the programme. The superconducting
condensate has an amplitude mode --- the Higgs mode, spectroscopically measured --- whose mass
$2\Delta$ is the cost of unbinding one Bell pair; whether the framework's $m_{H} = v/2$ admits a
pair-cost reading in the register is a question this paper logs and does not answer. The identity
between stiffness and entanglement suggests a third route to the boson-mass sector --- an
entanglement-stiffness computation on the register, beside the fibre geometry and the running ---
which would over-determine numbers this paper obtains once. And pair condensates with measured
off-diagonal long-range order have been prepared on quantum hardware by entangling qubit pairs,
which places an Anderson--Higgs demonstration --- the condensate built, the order witnessed
through its entanglement, the stiffness exhibited --- within reach of the same devices used in
\S\ref{sec:interactions}, and it has now been carried out: the twist response measured on a
Heron-class device, sign flip included, at visibility $0.87$ --- with the identity converting
the measured correlator into a measured tangle, so that the order parameter and the entanglement
measure arrived as one number (Appendix~\ref{app:hw-meissner}). What this section claims is the map; what it
does not claim is a derivation of superconductivity, a composite electroweak vacuum, or an answer
to the rhyme. The witness is called; the testimony is consonant; the cross-examination belongs to
the papers that follow.
