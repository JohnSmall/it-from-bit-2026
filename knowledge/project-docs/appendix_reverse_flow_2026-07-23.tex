\section{The dictionary read in reverse: areas for exploration}
\label{app:reverse-flow}

Everything quantitative in this paper runs in one direction: from the structure of
multipartite entanglement to the structure of the Standard Model. If the dictionary is
right, it is not a one-way street. Read through the same correspondence, every
empirically secure fact about the Standard Model becomes a statement about multipartite
entanglement --- and the Standard Model is the most precisely measured physical theory
ever constructed, while the theory of multipartite entanglement, for all the depth of its
classification results \autocite{durvidalcirac2000three, coeckekissinger2010compositional},
is young and short of organising principles. This appendix records the areas where the
reverse reading looks most likely to pay. Nothing below is a result, and nothing below is
even a well-posed problem; that is deliberate. Each area is stated at the level of
definiteness it actually possesses, with the literature a visitor would want, and the
field is left open.

One discipline governs the whole appendix. Where this paper \emph{derives} a
Standard-Model fact --- the gauge group, the charge assignments, the tree-level
$\sin^{2}\theta_{W}$ --- the reverse reading yields only a consistency check on the
derivation. The genuinely new content lives where the Standard-Model fact is empirically
secure but underived here: the dynamics of confinement, the running of the couplings, the
magnitude of \textsc{cp} violation, the existence of a gravitating dark sector. Those are
the facts treated below as generators.

\subsection{A string tension for entanglement extraction}
\label{app:rev-string}

Kinematic confinement is a theorem of the main text: the GHZ class has zero external
correlation budget and no singlet-direction access. The \emph{dynamical} face of
confinement has never been translated. In QCD, pulling colour charges apart does not
liberate them but creates pairs --- string breaking, observed on the lattice
\autocite{bali2005string}. The reverse reading suggests a dynamical counterpart: any
local protocol attempting to isolate one party of a GHZ-class state should either fail or
generate fresh GHZ triples, at a resource cost growing with the operational separation
--- a string tension for entanglement extraction. What ``separation'' means operationally
(circuit distance? the number of intervening FANOUT events?) is exactly the kind of
question we decline to settle here. A toy model exhibiting a linear cost law would
already be informative, and a proof that no such law can exist would locate precisely
where the dictionary's dynamical extension breaks. Monogamy
\autocite{coffmankunduwootters2000} supplies the kinematics; the dynamics is open
country.

\subsection{What confinement lets you broadcast}
\label{app:rev-broadcast}

Operationally, confinement is the statement that only colour-singlet combinations are
ever classically recorded. The main text reads classical recording as FANOUT and reads
the broadcastable content of a quantum description as an invariant ring. The natural
generalisation is that the FANOUT-broadcastable content of a multipartite entanglement
class is the invariant ring of that class's stabiliser. If true, this would refine the
no-broadcasting theorem \autocite{barnum1996broadcast} and its local-broadcasting
sharpening \autocite{pianihorodeckihorodecki2008local} class by class, with the GHZ
class --- whose stabiliser is explicit --- the obvious first case. Whether the
refinement holds, and whether it reproduces the quantum-Darwinism account of which
observables proliferate into records, are both unexplored.

\subsection{Anomaly cancellation as circuit bookkeeping}
\label{app:rev-anomaly}

The Standard Model's anomaly conditions --- per generation, $\sum Y = \sum Y^{3} = 0$
over the fermion content --- constrain which charges may \emph{coexist}, and 't~Hooft's
matching condition \autocite{thooft1980naturalness} makes the constraint stable under
change of scale. Pulled back through the dictionary, this suggests sum rules on closed
circuits: a reversible network with no external legs, decomposed into the Bell, GHZ and
W primitives whose compositional completeness Coecke and Kissinger proved
\autocite{coeckekissinger2010compositional}, should satisfy conservation laws over the
class invariants of its components, and the laws should survive blocking. An invariant
protected under both local operations and coarse-graining is exactly the tool the
many-body community wants for classifying phases by their entanglement; small closed ZX
diagrams are the obvious laboratory. Which invariants, and which decomposition, we do
not say --- in part because the charge assignments are derived rather than imported in
this paper, so the honest reverse content is the closed-circuit \emph{form} of the
constraint, not its coefficients.

\subsection{Beta functions as entanglement-renormalisation flow}
\label{app:rev-beta}

Asymptotic freedom \autocite{grosswilczek1973ultraviolet, politzer1973reliable} is not
derived in this paper, so the measured running of $\alpha_{s}$ is clean input. Read
backwards, it should predict how the density of genuinely tripartite correlation flows
under blocking in an entanglement-renormalisation scheme
\autocite{vidal2007renormalization}: logarithmically, with a coefficient fixed by
$b_{0} = 11 - \tfrac{2}{3}n_{f}$. Even the sign is untested --- does GHZ-type
correlation density grow toward the infrared, as confinement suggests, in any MERA whose
disentanglers are restricted to the dictionary's primitives? A numerical answer either
way would be the first contact between the renormalisation group of a gauge theory and
the renormalisation of entanglement made through this correspondence, rather than
through holography.

\subsection{A monotone calibrated in MeV}
\label{app:rev-monotone}

Multipartite entanglement lacks an agreed canonical measure; what any acceptable measure
must satisfy was axiomatised long ago \autocite{vidal2000monotones}. The mass functional
of this paper --- geodesic distance against the selected direction --- is a definite
functional on states whose spectrum on the physical classes is experimentally calibrated
at the per-cent level. The unexamined question is whether it is, or bounds, an
entanglement monotone: non-increasing under local operations and classical
communication. A numerical monotonicity scan over random states and random LOCC maps is
an afternoon's work, and either outcome is interesting. A pass would hand entanglement
theory a preferred multipartite measure with an experimentally measured spectrum; the
failure pattern of a fail --- which operations break monotonicity, and whether they
correspond to anything physical --- would be data about the dictionary itself.

\subsection{Charges as invariants, and the polytopes}
\label{app:rev-polytopes}

The three-tangle already separates the confined class from the free one. The dictionary
suggests that \emph{every} conserved charge of the Standard Model should pull back to a
computable invariant of entanglement classes, with charge quantisation appearing as the
prediction that the invariants take discrete spectra on physical configurations. The
natural home for this is the entanglement-polytope programme
\autocite{klyachko2006marginal, walter2013polytopes}, in which one-body marginal spectra
classify multiparticle entanglement --- through the dictionary, precisely single-particle
quantum numbers classifying particle content. The rigidity of the Standard Model's
charge assignments would then translate into predicted vertex structure for higher-party
polytopes, in a regime where the polytopes are largely uncomputed.

\subsection{Flavour as a measured chart of the four-qubit landscape}
\label{app:rev-flavour}

Four qubits can be entangled in nine different ways \autocite{verstraete2002four}, and
no four-qubit state is maximally entangled across every bipartition
\autocite{higuchisudbery2000, hubersiewert2026}. The companion programme flagged in the
interactions section runs the forward direction: mixing forced by the AME deficit,
generations from the sedenion doubling \autocite{gillardgresnigt2019three}. The reverse
direction is available to anyone, with no commitment to that programme: take the
measured Wolfenstein and PMNS parameters as experimental data \emph{about} the geometry
of the four-qubit landscape near the frustration optimum, ask which of the nine families
the flavour configuration inhabits, and let that family's invariants return predictions.
On this reading, flavour physics is the most precisely measured four-party entanglement
experiment ever performed, and nobody has yet looked at it as one.

\subsection{CP violation as imaginarity}
\label{app:rev-cp}

The magnitude of \textsc{cp} violation is not derived here, so it too is clean input.
Its invariant form is Jarlskog's rephasing-invariant quartic
\autocite{jarlskog1985commutator}, and its natural entanglement-theoretic home is the
young resource theory of imaginarity \autocite{hickeygour2018imaginarity,
wu2021imaginarity}, which quantifies what a state's description needs beyond the reals.
The area: whether the Jarlskog quartic is the canonical conjugation-sensitive monotone
at four parties, and whether the chirality of physically realisable four-party
configurations is bounded by the unitarity maximum $J \leq 1/(6\sqrt{3})$. Any progress
here would also bear on the chirality question this paper leaves open in the dictionary
lemmas.

\subsection{Electroweak decoherence spectroscopy}
\label{app:rev-ew}

This paper reads mass as debt, and debt as fragility. The photon is the unbroken,
massless combination of the electroweak quartet; the $Z$ is the broken, massive one.
Read backwards: the two orthogonal combinations of bipartite correlation at the
$\mathbb{C}\otimes\mathbb{H}$ level should decohere at systematically different rates,
with the unbroken combination protected and the ratio tied to $\cos\theta_{W}$. We know
no mechanism theorem linking mass to decoherence rate, and we flag the idea at
correspondingly low status; but the experiment requires no theory at all. Prepare the
quartet structure on any noisy processor, track both combinations under the native
noise, and see whether the unbroken direction lives longer. It is the kind of
measurement a small hardware notebook could perform this year.

\subsection{Bound entanglement and the gauge-blind sector}
\label{app:rev-bound}

In the dictionary a gauge boson is a bipartite (Bell-class) edge, so distillable
entanglement --- extractable Bell pairs \autocite{horodecki1998bound} --- is precisely
gauge-visible correlation. Bound entanglement, entangled but undistillable, is then
correlation with no gauge edge available: matter that correlates without coupling to any
gauge channel --- structurally dark. The geometry of the separable and bound sets
\autocite{zyczkowski1998volume} thereby acquires an unexpected phenomenological
interest. Is bound entanglement generic at many parties, as the dark sector's abundance
would suggest? Does the known activation of bound entanglement have a portal analogue?
We record the rhyme and nothing more.

\subsection{A confining sector as a quantum memory}
\label{app:rev-memory}

Confinement plus a mass gap, read as code properties: logical information stored in the
gauge-invariant sector, non-singlet errors energetically penalised, and the logical
operators the colour-singlet composites --- a baryon as a weight-three composite of GHZ
primitives, a meson as a GHZ primitive against its conjugate. Whether such a sector is a
self-correcting quantum memory at finite temperature is then a sharp question aimed at a
famous one: self-correction is established in four dimensions
\autocite{dennis2002topological} and open or obstructed below
\autocite{brown2016memories}. The dictionary at least says where to look: networks of
GHZ primitives under the budget-saturation constraint of the main text. A sharp negative
would illuminate both sides.

\subsection{The vertex set as a gate library}
\label{app:rev-gates}

One beachhead already exists in the literature, found from the physics side: low-energy
strong-interaction scattering suppresses entanglement generation, and the suppression
correlates with an emergent symmetry \autocite{beane2019entanglement}. The dictionary
suggests the systematic version. The images of the Standard-Model vertices form a small
gate library, and their entangling powers \autocite{zanardizalkafaoro2000entangling}
should sit at values fixed by the framework's constants --- the fractions $\tfrac{2}{9}$
and $\tfrac14$ recur throughout this paper, and computing the entangling power of the
vertex gates against them is a day's numerics. If the values land, the Standard Model's
interaction strengths acquire an operational meaning inside quantum information; if they
do not, the vertex-image construction is wrong in an instructive way.

\subsection{Symmetry breaking as a measurement-induced transition}
\label{app:rev-mipt}

The haziest entry, recorded because two communities are adjacent and apparently unaware
of each other. Measurement-induced phase transitions \autocite{lichenfisher2018zeno,
skinnerruhmannahum2019measurement} tune between entangling unitaries and disentangling
measurements; FANOUT density is this framework's native version of the tuning parameter.
The electroweak vacuum, in the dictionary, is a condensate of the product class. The
guess: the Standard Model's breaking pattern predicts the order-parameter structure of a
measurement-induced transition --- condensation of the product class with a residual
unbroken correlation subgroup --- and possibly a critical FANOUT density expressible in
the framework's constants. We can defend no part of this beyond the observation that
both subjects are, at bottom, about where entanglement goes when copying wins.

\subsection*{Coda}

None of the above carries a status above fenced rhyme, and none is posed; that is the
point. The forward direction of this paper took two decades because the dictionary had
to be built before it could be read. The reverse direction requires only that the
dictionary be read the other way, and it can be read by anyone. We would be glad to hear
from readers who take any of these up --- and gladder still to be told which of them
fail.
