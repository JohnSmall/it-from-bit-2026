# The Fourth Qubit: CKM and PMNS from the Sedenion Doubling

**Session document, 2026-07-06.** Records an idea of JS's from today's session, its grounding, the
corrections and conjectures that emerged in discussion, the risk items, and the programme. Status
vocabulary as usual: proved / structural / conjecture / open. **Housing decision (JS):** this is
the seed of a dedicated follow-up paper; Paper 1 carries only a brief provenance mention in the
interactions section and a forward sentence in the Cabibbo open problem. This document goes to the
project documents so the idea is not lost.

## 1. Provenance

Two threads met today. First, the old blockage: the Cabibbo angle was executed as a circuit
(R_y(2·2/9) on a generation qubit, per-cent agreement, hardware-confirmed), but the full CKM was
never attempted because the generation wire was an unlicensed pragmatic device — an extra qubit
with no rung in the register architecture. Second, the fresh theorem: the leptonic ninth (the W
register's unspent 1/9 of entanglement budget per qubit, with external pairwise correlation bounded
by exactly that ninth, and the quark register offering exactly zero). JS's conjecture, verbatim in
spirit: the full CKM's complex phase "signals something outside"; an extra qubit could provide it;
that means going up to the sedenions, which contain three copies of the octonions; so perhaps the
three generations come from a fourth qubit that uses the leftover W-state budget.

## 2. The exact identity

Four qubits are (ℂ²)⊗⁴ = ℂ¹⁶ = **ℂ ⊗ 𝕊**, the complex sedenions — exactly as the three-qubit
register is ℂ⁸ = ℂ ⊗ 𝕆 (the Bernevig–Chen arena the paper already inhabits). The fourth qubit does
not approximate the sedenion doubling; it **is** the sedenion doubling, one Cayley–Dickson rung
past the register. Status: arithmetic, exact.

## 3. Algebraic grounding (search-verified 2026-07-06)

- **Aut(𝕊) = Aut(𝕆) × S₃ = G₂ × S₃** (Brown's theorem). The only difference between the octonion
  and sedenion automorphism groups is the permutation factor S₃.
- In the Gresnigt line (Gillard & Gresnigt 2019, `gillardgresnigt2019three`, **already cited in
  Paper 1's zoo section**), the three generations are **three intersecting octonion subalgebras**
  of 𝕊, with the S₃ automorphisms generating the second and third generations from the first.
- The triple intersection **O₁ ∩ O₂ ∩ O₃ = ℍ**, generated by {e₀, e₄, e₈, e₁₂}: the three
  generations share a quaternionic core. Framework resonance: generations share their electroweak
  (ℍ-rung) machinery and differ octonionically — precisely the register's shape.
- The S₃ can be constructed from the **triality automorphism of Spin(8)** — connecting the
  sedenion route directly to the triality-breaking already used for chirality in Paper 1.
- Literature sentiment: Cayley–Dickson algebras beyond 𝕊 "only add additional trialities, i.e.
  copies", with reasonably no new physics beyond sedenions.

## 4. The safety rail (flavour, not force)

The Hopf tower terminates (Adams) and the normed division algebras terminate (Hurwitz); 𝕊 has zero
divisors, so there is no fibration S³¹ → S¹⁶. Consequence: the fourth qubit **cannot extend the
gauge tower** — it can carry flavour structure but never a force. Paper 1's exclusion theorems,
which forbid a fourth gauge sector, are the new mechanism's guarantee rather than its obstacle.
Status: theorem (the termination) + structural reading.

## 5. Conjecture 1 — mixing is forced (the AME shadow)

No four-qubit state is maximally entangled across every bipartition (Higuchi–Sudbery 2000; seven
proofs collected in Huber–Siewert 2026 — both keys delivered to the library today,
`higuchisudbery2000`, `hubersiewert2026`). Therefore a fourth-party generation tag can **never be
perfectly correlated** with the register triple; generations cannot superselect; **mixing is
obligatory**. The CKM and PMNS structure as the operational shadow of the sedenion zero divisors —
the same obstruction seen algebraically (zero divisors), information-theoretically (no AME(4,2)),
and phenomenologically (nontrivial mixing). Target: bound the mixing angles by the AME-deficit
geometry. This also merges naturally with the programme's standing AME(4,2)-via-zero-divisors
open-problem item: the two were always one object. Status: conjecture, the sharpest of the idea.

## 6. Conjecture 2 — the CKM/PMNS size asymmetry (the budget postdiction)

The correction to the original guess, which upgrades it. The leftover ninth is **leptonic**: by the
budget theorem, each W-register qubit can carry external pairwise correlation up to 1/9, while the
quark register (1 = 0 + 0 + 1) offers **zero external room of any kind**. Hence the fourth qubit
can couple to leptons at the **state level** (within the ninth), but to quarks only at the **frame
level** (the resolution classes; consistent with turned-never-held). Consequence, if it holds:
lepton mixing is loose and large (state-coupled tags), quark mixing is small and rigidly
hierarchical (frame-coupled tags) — the observed **PMNS-large / CKM-small asymmetry**, one of
flavour physics' brutest unexplained facts, falling out of the confinement budget. Target shape for
the quark side: the Wolfenstein hierarchy in powers of λ = sin(2/9), whose first rung
(|V_ud|, |V_us|) Paper 1 has already executed and passed. Status: structural candidate.

## 7. The phase, in the framework's own doctrine

"Contextuality" transposes into the relational-phase doctrine Paper 1 already runs on: a flavour
phase is gauge until there is something to compare it against. The physical object is the
rephasing-invariant (Jarlskog's J); the fourth party is the anchor that promotes δ_CP from
convention to physics — *the Wigner's-friend phase of the flavour sector*, existing only from
above. Open tension, recorded honestly: if the quark tag is frame-coupled, where does the quark
sector's CP anchor live? Candidate directions: through the lepton-side anchor jointly; through the
multipartite residual channel; or the phase as a property of the tagging *map* rather than any
state. Unresolved.

## 8. Risk items (check these first)

1. **Non-associativity of sedenion left actions.** Gresnigt's construction routes through Clifford
   left-multiplication algebras precisely because compositions of sedenion left actions misbehave;
   whether the plain four-qubit tensor structure matches their ℂℓ routing is CHECK #1 of the
   follow-up paper. If it does not, the identity of §2 is necessary but not sufficient.
2. **Two dimensions, three generations.** A qubit carries at most the 2-dimensional S₃ irrep; three
   generations organise as 1 ⊕ 2. The tag need not be three basis labels — frame-level tagging and
   the 1 ⊕ 2 split are both live designs — but this is a design decision, not yet a derivation.
3. **Is a ninth enough?** Quantify how much generation distinguishability (trace distance between
   tag-conditional states) fits inside the leptonic 1/9. A crisp computable curve; if the answer is
   "not enough for the observed PMNS", Conjecture 2's lepton side weakens early and cheaply.

## 10. Deliberately not claimed (dead-route prophylaxis)

Not a fourth force (excluded by termination — the point, not a loss). Not a revival of quarks as
internal GHZ/W superpositions (retired 2026-07-06; species are class-discrete; admixture remains
the localized up-quark candidate only). Not Kochen–Specker contextuality (the claim is relational-
phase, §7). Not state-level quark tagging (excluded by the budget theorem — and the exclusion is
Conjecture 2's engine, not its embarrassment).
