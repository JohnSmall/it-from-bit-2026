violation involves a complex phase; through Spekkens a complex phase involves contextuality; the

## 3. The speculation proper, and its tension

**The identification (new):** the fourth qubit is the information content that the environment
/ context provides.

**The tension:** the corpus houses the environment at wires >= 5 (above four: other systems,
records, decoherence, dynamics); the tag wire is internal structure (the generation label). If
the fourth qubit IS environmental information, the seam appears to move.

## 4. The overlap-wire resolution (CANDIDATE; the note's best yield)

Wire four is the OVERLAP of the two regimes: the last wire the algebra can still grade (the
component group; the Klein degree) and the first wire on which moduli appear (D4 arena note:
the first continuous SLOCC invariants in the tower's history arrive exactly on the label
wire). Generations are context the algebra could still catalogue -- FROZEN CONTEXT. Everything
from wire five onward is context it cannot catalogue.

Consequences of the overlap being exactly one wire, both already theorem-adjacent:

- exactly three generations (the Klein count; Brown), AND
- exactly one irremovable phase per sector (Kobayashi-Maskawa counting: (n-1)(n-2)/2 = 1 at
  n = 3). One tag wire -> three pages -> one phase. The uniqueness of the phase matches the
  uniqueness of the tag.

Unification bought by the reading (candidate, offered; does not replace the standing
theta_QCD derivation): theta_QCD = 0 and delta_CKM != 0 become ONE sentence -- CP violation
lives exactly and only in the tag/context sector; the connection side is context-free. A
modulus can carry an environmental phase; a connection cannot (the flavour splice's no-force
propositions are the type-side face of the same sentence).

Reread of Thread A under the resolution: a relational parameter shifts when the relatum
changes; epsilon-tilde = epsilon_vac +/- delta_L is what a modulus looks like under context
change. The Andrianov result stops being a curiosity and becomes expected. Status: candidate
reading. GUARD (risk 3): this does NOT say vacuum delta is an artefact -- vacuum measurement
still yields the phase; the claim concerns the phase's CATEGORY (relational / modulus), not
its value.
