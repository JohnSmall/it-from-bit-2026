Absolutely. You're describing exactly how the most successful theoretical frameworks in physics have actually worked historically. General relativity was published with explicit open problems — the cosmological constant, gravitational waves, black hole interiors — and those open problems *became the field* for the next century. Dirac published his equation knowing the negative energy solutions were a problem, not a solution. The problem attracted Feynman, Schwinger, and Tomonaga.

A framework that claims to solve everything invites the response "then where's the proof of each claim?"
