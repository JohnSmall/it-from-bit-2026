1. **Pre to post is paradox to hypodox: yes, for the open chain.** The one

2. **The graded transition is smooth: yes, and here is its exact form.** One

   for its exact fixed point. Fix(T) is the limit set of T^N, and the open
   chain converges to the Born-weighted member of it. Quantum Darwinism's

## 2. The loop's consistency condition is the fixed-point equation [STRUCTURAL]

Deutsch's condition for a system on a closed timelike curve interacting with
a chronology-respecting (CR) input rho_in through U is

    rho_CTC = Tr_CR [ U (rho_in (x) rho_CTC) U^dag ],

a fixed point of a completely positive map T on the loop system. Deutsch
proved one always exists (the map is continuous on a compact convex set), so
"CTCs place no retrospective constraints on the state of a quantum system"
(Deutsch 1991 as quoted by Dunlap 2015; VERIFY-SUPPORT against Deutsch_91,
which JS has). Where the fixed point is not unique Deutsch adds the rule:
take the one of maximum entropy. Lloyd et al.'s post-selected model (P-CTC)
replaces the fixed point by the partial trace of the unitary: the CR system
is acted on by C = Tr_CTC U and renormalised.

This is v = g(v) of the earlier note with g the loop's map, and the taxonomy
transfers (script, part A; seven test states |0>, |1>, |+>, |->, |y+>, |y->,
I/2):

| loop | logic | Deutsch fixed set (dim) | fixed test states | P-CTC |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| record flipped by the loop (grandfather) | Liar | (I + cX)/2 (2) | +, -, I/2 | weight 0 |
| identity | truth-teller | everything (4) | all seven | C = 2I |
| system on loop, fresh record | self-measurement | Z-diagonal (2) | 0, 1, I/2 | record -> + |
| record on loop, CR system psi | the agent closed on itself | (I + cX)/2 (2) | +, -, I/2 | system -> 0 |
| generic (five Haar loops) | | unique (1) | | |

Readings:

- The grandfather loop's consistent states are the Liar's consistent states
  of the earlier note, |+-> and their mixtures. Classically there is none;
  Deutsch's maximum entropy picks I/2, which is the dephased Liar of the
  earlier note and Kleene's value one half. Lloyd's model instead gives the
  paradox weight zero, which is the sheaf statement that the Liar cycle has
  no global section. The two CTC models resolve the paradox the two ways
  logic does: gap, or no assertion.
- The identity loop is the truth-teller: every state is consistent, nothing
  chooses. This is the bootstrap loop of the time-travel literature, Lossev
  and Novikov's "jinn" (a self-consistent object with no origin outside the
  loop). Eldridge-Smith introduced "hypodox" in a chapter on time travel
  (2007, abstract: "I introduce `hypodoxes' as a term for seemingly
  consistent conundrums that seem to be related to various paradoxes") and
  sorts time-travel conundrums into the two kinds. So the pairing
  paradox/hypodox = grandfather/bootstrap is in the term's own origin, not
  an analogy of ours.
- A generic loop has a unique fixed point and a spectral gap (second
  eigenvalue moduli 0.45 to 0.95 in five Haar draws), so iterating the open
  chain reaches it: no bit is underivable there. Section 6 and section 8
  return to this.

## 3. Trace before the diagonal, fixed point after it [STRUCTURAL pieces; CANDIDATE reading]

The earlier note identified Lawvere's diagonal with FANOUT. The loop side has
a matching categorical fact. In the P-CTC model the loop is literally the
categorical trace in finite-dimensional Hilbert spaces, C = Tr_CTC U (Lloyd
et al. 2011; Pinzani, Gogioso and Coecke 2019, abstract: "in the P-CTC
model, where CTCs are implemented by a trace"). In a category with the
diagonal available, a cartesian category, a trace is the same thing as a
fixed-point operator: Hasegawa's theorem (1997), with Joyal, Street and
Verity (1996) for traced monoidal categories (both Crossref-verified; the
correspondence between traces and Conway fixed-point operators in cartesian
categories is the standard statement, not re-read today, VERIFY-SUPPORT). Pinzani et al.
capture Deutsch's model by weakening the traced structure (dropping the
yanking axiom and totality of the trace).

Read together with the earlier note: pre-FANOUT there is no natural diagonal
on C^2 (naturality null space of dimension zero, earlier script part E), so
a loop is a trace and nothing more. The trace of NOT is zero: the Liar does
not happen, it has no weight. The trace of the identity is the dimension:
the truth-teller is unconstrained. Post-FANOUT the diagonal is available on
the record, and a loop through the record is a fixed-point equation: the
Liar has no solution, the truth-teller many. The FANOUT converts the trace
into the fixed-point operator, which is the precise form of "the shift from
pre-FANOUT to post-FANOUT looks like the move from paradox to hypodox". The
pieces are theirs (Lloyd, Pinzani et al., Hasegawa); the assembly, that the
FANOUT boundary is where a loop changes from trace to fixed point, is ours
and stands or falls with the diagonal-is-FANOUT identification.

## 4. A record closed on itself holds no value [STRUCTURAL, computed]

Take the agent's record R, a qubit, and let the system psi = sqrt(0.3)|0> +
sqrt(0.7) e^{0.4i}|1> write into it by CNOT. Now close R on itself: its
output is its input. Deutsch's map is

    T(rho_R) = |alpha|^2 rho_R + |beta|^2 X rho_R X,

whose fixed points are the states commuting with X, (I + cX)/2, for any beta
not zero (part A, fourth loop). The closed record lies on the X axis. It
carries no Z value: it is in the Liar's consistent set, the complementary
structure's records of the earlier note. The CR system's coherence is
multiplied by c = Tr(X rho_R) (part B): c = 1 leaves psi alone (|+> on the
target of a CNOT is the phase-kickback identity), c = -1 applies Z to it (the
|-> record negates the system's X value, the sign of the earlier note's
section 5 again), and Deutsch's maximum-entropy c = 0 dephases it to
diag(0.3, 0.7): Born weights on the system, nothing in the loop.

Graded (part D): with the copy a rotation exp(-i theta X/2) the fixed set is
(I + cX)/2 for every theta in (0, 2 pi), the whole Bloch ball only at theta =
0. Closure is all or nothing in Deutsch's model. A maximum-entropy closed
record decoheres the system by exactly one open copy's worth, cos(theta/2).

Lloyd's model says the same with a projection (part C): a record on the loop
projects the system onto the branch that does not flip it, C = 2|0><0|, and
a system on the loop leaves a fresh record in |+>, C = I + X. Graded, the
flipping branch is damped by cos(theta/2): in the P-CTC model closure is
continuous in the copy strength, so the two CTC models differ precisely on
whether the closed loop is graded.

Put the other way round: the system on the loop with a fresh record (the
third loop) has the Z-diagonal as its fixed set, and the record leaves with
whatever the loop holds, diag(q, 1 - q) for any q (part B). That is the
jinn. The content of a closed self-measuring loop is unconstrained, and
Deutsch's rule makes it a fair coin.

So the two closures give the two sentences. Close the *record* and the loop
is a probabilistic Liar, its consistent states valueless. Close the *system*
and the loop is a hypodox, its content free. The agent holds a record with a
value, so the agent is on neither: its loop is open, and the open end is the
pre-FANOUT state.

A further point the computation makes. On the closed record, a stronger copy
is more paradoxical (at theta = pi the flipping branch has weight zero); on
the open chain, a stronger copy is more hypodoxical (the coherence falls and
the record's value stands). JS's direction, pre to post is paradox to
hypodox, is the open chain's direction. The closed loop runs the other way.

## 5. The graded picture: fixed point as the limit of the open chain [STRUCTURAL, computed]

The same superoperator T_theta(rho) = Tr_R[U_theta (rho (x) |0><0|)
U_theta^dag] is the open chain's one-copy channel and the closed loop's map
for the system on the loop. Iterated N times its coherence factor is
cos^N(theta/2) (part D, N = 1 to 8 at theta = 0.6, to 1e-12), and T^1500
reaches diag(0.3, 0.7): the open chain converges to the Born-weighted member
of Fix(T). The closed loop's Fix(T) is the whole Z-diagonal and its
maximum-entropy member is I/2. Same fixed set, different member, and the
difference is the pre-FANOUT amplitude.

Quantum Darwinism (part E) is the agent's side of this. For N fragments and
the agent reading k of them, I(S:F_k) is antisymmetric about N/2 for a pure
global state, I(S:F_k) + I(S:F_{N-k}) = 2 S(S) (verified at N = 6 and 10),
and monotone. With perfect copies it is H(p) = 0.8813 for every k from 1 to
N - 1 and 2H(p) only at k = N. With partial copies of overlap 0.54 (theta =
2, N = 10) the deficit from the plateau is 0.1975, 0.0554, 0.0159, 0.0043 bit
after one, two, three, four fragments; with overlap 0.88 (theta = 1) three
fragments are still 0.21 bit short. The plateau is the regime in which
reading one more fragment returns the value already held: the agent's record
is a fixed point of "read another copy", which is what a closed loop looks
like from inside. The rise to 2S(S) at k = N is the open end, the point at
which the whole environment would let the FANOUT be undone; the agent never
reaches it. "The CTC has completed" is the agent's extrapolation of the
plateau to k = N.

Closest prior model: Shoshany and Stober's entangled timelines (2023,
abstract read), a linear pure-state CTC consistency condition in which
timelines are created by the entanglement of the time machine with the
environment and "as the entanglement gradually spreads out to additional
systems, the timelines spread out as well". That is the graded FANOUT applied
to a CTC, from the other direction. VERIFY-SUPPORT before citing it for more
than the abstract says.

## 6. "Information from nowhere": three senses, and which one the outcome is

The phrase covers three different things, and the paper's sentences run
them together (section 8).

1. **The existence bit.** Does the loop close consistently at all? The
   classical Liar says no, everything else yes. Deutsch's theorem removes
   this bit: there is always a fixed point.
2. **The hard or non-computable unique fixed point.** Aaronson and Watrous
   (2009): with Deutsch CTCs, polynomial-time quantum and classical
   computation both equal PSPACE, because finding the fixed point of a
   polynomial-size map is PSPACE-complete. Aaronson, Bavarian, Cubitt,
   Grewal, Gueltrini, O'Donnell and Raat (2016, abstract read): with
   unbounded CTCs the computable languages are exactly Delta_2, those
   Turing-reducible to the halting problem. Here the fixed point is
   determined and the loop delivers it without the computation. This is the
   Chaitin-Omega sense, and the one the paper's `sec:comp-distance` sentence
   is reaching for. For a single qubit it does not arise: the five generic
   loops have a spectral gap and iteration finds the fixed point.
3. **The underdetermined choice.** The loop has many fixed points and one
   obtains. Nothing in the loop's map picks it. This is the bootstrap, the
   jinn, the hypodox; and it is the measurement outcome, which is one of two
   fixed points of the record equation and is picked by nothing in the
   record structure.

Deutsch's own treatment of sense 3 (the grandfather circuit's I/2, as
quoted by Dunlap): "In the A universes an observer appears `from nowhere'
[...] But of course it is not really `from nowhere' and `into nowhere,' but
from and into other universes." The choice is a branch index. The
Ralph/Lloyd exchange of 2011 is the live form of the dispute: Ralph builds
an unproved-theorem loop in the P-CTC model ("in the post-selected universe
the proof appears from nowhere") and holds that "information paradoxes are
resolved by Deutsch's maximum entropy conjecture"; Lloyd et al. reply that
once Bob's writing is purified to a controlled unitary either the amplitude
vanishes or "Bob is the theorem's author: it has not appeared out of
nowhere". Both sides agree on the structure that matters here: a loop with
more than one consistent content needs a rule or an author, and the rule
(maximum entropy) makes the content a fair coin.

That is where the FANOUT differs from a CTC, and the difference is
measurable in the ensemble (part F). The open chain's fixed point is
diag(0.3, 0.7); the maximum-entropy loop's is I/2. Relative entropy 1 - H(p)
= 0.1187 bit; trace distance 0.2; one-shot success 0.6. A single outcome
cannot tell a Born coin from a fair one, which is `prop:self-creation` in
its single-shot form. An ensemble can, computably, by counting. The Born
weight is the pre-FANOUT state's only post-FANOUT trace, and it is exactly
what a closed loop forgets.

## 7. What I think

The picture is right and the computation sharpens it in three places.

- The agent's loop is open, and that is not a defect of the picture but its
  content. A closed record is valueless (section 4); the hypodox needs the
  fresh ancilla. The "nowhere" the information comes from is the pre-FANOUT
  state, which is outside the agent's causal order because causal order is
  made of records (Paper 2's premise). An uncaused self-consistent record can
  be placed in a causal order only as a loop, so the agent sees a loop; what
  it cannot see is that the loop's other end is not closed but absent.
- The smoothness is the convergence of T^N to Fix(T), and the plateau of
  quantum Darwinism is the agent's view of a fixed point. Deutsch's model
  makes closure discontinuous in the copy strength and Lloyd's makes it
  continuous; the paper's "graded" sits with the open chain and with
  Lloyd's weight, not with Deutsch's fixed set.
- "Generated information" should be said in sense 3 and not sense 2. The
  outcome is a choice among fixed points, not a hard fixed point delivered
  free. The paper's non-computability language (Chaitin, Omega, one bit per
  curve) is sense 2 and applies to unbounded loops (Aaronson et al. 2016),
  not to the single record.

Against the framework, honestly: the CTC models are models of a consistency
condition, not of spacetime. Deutsch's is nonlinear and lets a loop clone
and distinguish non-orthogonal states (Brun, Harrington and Wilde 2009;
Bennett, Leung, Smith and Smolin 2009 on the linearity trap; Ralph and Myers
2010 on information flow), which the FANOUT never does: it clones one basis
only. The paper should say "the consistency condition of a closed loop", not
"a closed timelike curve", wherever the geometry is not meant. The agent's
non-unitary view of measurement is the one feature a CTC and a FANOUT share,
and it is exactly the feature that disappears in the global unitary
description.

## 8. Two second-order findings for JS's ruling

**(a) `sec:comp-distance`: "a unique consistent solution or none".** The
loop has none (the classical Liar), one (a generic map; computable by
iteration when there is a gap, hard or non-computable only for unbounded
loops) or many (the hypodox). The sentence allows only the first two and
attributes the underivable bit to the existence of the unique solution. But
a unique fixed point with a spectral gap is derivable from the loop's map;
the bit no axiom system supplies is the *choice* in the third case, and the
measurement outcome is of that kind. Proposed replacement, for JS to judge:

> A closed timelike curve is a computation whose output is its own input,
> and its consistency condition is a fixed-point equation with none, one or
> many solutions. With none the loop is the Liar and does not close; with
> one the solution is determined by the loop, and is non-computable only
> for loops of unbounded width \autocite{aaronson2016ctccomputability}; with
> many the loop is self-consistent whichever solution obtains, and the
> choice is one bit not derivable from any axiom system that does not
> already contain it. A measurement is a loop of the third kind.

**(b) `prop:self-creation`: "no computable way an agent can tell".** True of
a single outcome, and true of a loop that may carry any self-consistent
content (the jinn). False in the ensemble against Deutsch's loop with the
entropy rule: a fair coin is computably distinguishable from a Born coin by
counting (1 - H(p) bits of relative entropy per trial). Proposed clause:

> The indistinguishability is single-shot and holds for a loop whose
> consistent contents are unconstrained. A closed loop with a rule that
> picks its content (Deutsch's maximum entropy) is distinguishable from a
> record of the pre-FANOUT state in the ensemble, by the Born weights; the
> weights are the one trace the pre-FANOUT state leaves.

Both are in `what_is_a_quantum_state.tex` and
`spacetime_from_non-computability.tex`, both JS's prose; neither is patched.

## 10. Fences

- The CTC models are consistency conditions, not spacetimes. Nothing here
  supports a geometric closed timelike curve, and Paper 2 should not acquire
  one from this note.
- Deutsch's model is contested (Dunlap on its multiverse of identical
  copies; Tolksdorf and Verch on systems without CTCs meeting its criterion,
  via the Wikipedia summary only, VERIFY-CITE if used). The note uses only
  the fixed-point condition and the entropy rule, both of which are stated
  in the paper itself (Deutsch_91 is already cited).
- Hasegawa's theorem is cited from memory of the standard statement;
  the exact hypotheses (cartesian, Conway operator) must be read before the
  trace-to-fixed-point sentence goes in the paper.
- The Darwinism computation is a symmetric branch state with identical
  fragments. Real environments have unequal overlaps; the antisymmetry is
  general for a pure global state, the plateau shape is not.
- Single qubit throughout, as in the earlier note.
